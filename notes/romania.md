  17 is the *additional* clubs. Gen 1 §117, §187 and Gen 2 §261 still say "seventeen"; fix on those passes.)
- **Grigore** Caracostea (NOT "Gregore") — played for Racing Club de France; founded the **Central
  Commission of Football-Rugby in 1915**; RRF formally established **26 March 1931**, Caracostea first leader.
- **First international: June 1919**, Paris, Inter-Allied Games (Games ran 22 June–6 July). **USA 21–0
  Romania.** The oft-cited "26 June" is NOT corroborated — sources only bracket it within the Games.
  Some sources also record a Romania–France loss at the same Games (53–16 *or* 48–5) — usually filed unofficial.
- **Paris 1924 Olympics**, 3 entrants (France, USA, Romania), Stade Yves-de-Manoir, Colombes:
  - **4 May 1924: France 59–3 Romania** (13 tries, 4 to **Adolphe Jaureguy** — at Stade Français from
    1923, so "Stade Français winger" is correct for 1924). **Olympedia gives 61–3** — discrepancy flagged in prose.

  - 18 May 1924: USA 17–3 France. Romania **bronze** (3 entrants).
  - Totals: Romania conceded **96** (59+37), scored 3.
- **World Rugby Hall of Fame:** the **1924 Romania Olympic Team** inducted **2012**; still Romania's
  **only** inductees as of 2026.
- Rugby was an Olympic sport in 4 editions (1900, 1908, 1920, 1924) — confirmed.
- Romania's **first win over France was 1960 (11–5)**, not 1974 — relevant to Gen 0's forward-refs
  (Gen 0 now says "beat Wales and Scotland in the 1980s", dropping the France claim). See Gen 3 flags.
