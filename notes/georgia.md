## Thesis
Rugby maps onto the folk mass-ball game **lelo (burti)**, giving it deep popular roots — the opposite
of an imported elite sport. The "control experiment": same sport, no class ceiling, because it entered
through a left-wing/French channel into a Soviet republic that already had a village version.

## Generations (established)
- **Gen 0 — Lelo Burti (Antiquity–1927).** Folk mass-ball game.
- **Gen 1 — The Armenian from Marseille (1928–1963).** Haspekian; the false starts.
- **Gen 2 — The Soviet Machine (1964–1988).** No national team; Georgians as USSR hard men.
- **Gen 3 — Independence and Ruin (1989–1996).** Kutaisi 1989; two balls & tractors.
- **Gen 4 — The French Connection (1997–2006).** Saurel exports the squad to France.
- **Gen 5 — The Billionaire (2007–2018).** Ivanishvili/Cartu; dominance; union crisis coda.
- **Gen 6 — The Locked Door and the Scandal (2019–2026).** Black Lion; Six Nations case; doping scandal.

## Verified facts (fact-check pass, July 2026)
- **"Lelo" = the Georgian word for a try.** National team = "the Lelos." Chant "Lelo, Lelo, Sakartvelo." ✓
- **Lelo burti ball weighs ~16 kg** (2023 ball ~17.5 kg), soft black leather **stuffed with earth,
  sand and sawdust** (and soaked with wine in the stuffing ceremony). *[CORRECTION: chapter had said
  "2.5–7 kg / grass, horsehair, sheep's wool" — was wrong; fixed the "seven-kilo sun" motif to
  "sixteen-kilo" throughout.]* Played Easter Sunday in **Shukhuti, Guria**, between Upper (Zemo) and
  Lower (Kvemo) Shukhuti; **priest carries/blesses the ball, shotgun starts it**; winners place the
  ball on the grave of a villager who died in the past year. Historic belief: winning village gets the
  **better harvest**. Folklore ties it to solar worship ("lelo" possibly ← Sumerian sun-god "Lil").
- **Appears in Rustaveli's 12th-c. epic *The Knight in the Panther's Skin*** (as *burtaoba*). ✓
- **Jacques Haspekian** — Armenian from Marseille — first session **15 Oct 1959, Tbilisi racecourse,
  20 attendees**. Still alive; interviewed on French radio when Georgia played France at RWC 2007. ✓
- **First Test: 12 Sept 1989, Kutaisi, Georgia 16–3 Zimbabwe.** Captain **Oleg Liparteliani** (centre);
  fly-half **David Dzagnidze** scored all the points. *[Note: "Nugzar Dragnidze" in an earlier draft
  was unreliable — likely a garble of the "N. Dzagnidze" in the lineup; removed.]*
- **1999 RWC repechage vs Tonga:** lost 1st leg **37–6** (Nukuʻalofa), won 2nd **28–27** (Tbilisi),
  out on aggregate. ✓  Georgia have reached **every RWC since 2003**.
- **2007 RWC:** beat Namibia **30–0** (first RWC win); lost to **Ireland 14–10** (near-upset). ✓
- **Ivanishvili / Cartu Foundation:** ~**£80 million** into Georgian rugby; **14 high-performance
  centres** built across the country. ✓  Rugby Europe Championship won **17 times** (first 2001).
- **2020–21 GRU presidential crisis:** Irakli **Abuseridze** elected Dec 2020, rejected by government
  ("procedural violations"); **Soso (Ioseb) Tkemaladze** — Ivanishvili associate — elected March 2021
  as sole candidate; violent clashes between supporters, injuries. ✓
- **Beat Wales (2022, Cardiff) and Italy;** **Lobzhanidze** = youngest ever in a RWC match (2015). ✓
- **Doping scandal ("Operation Obsidian"), 13 Mar 2026:** joint WADA/World Rugby probe; **6 players +
  1 support-staff** sanctioned; **GADA** (Georgia's anti-doping agency) tipped players off re: testing
  via text → team group chat; DCOs failed to witness urine passing; falsified test dates/docs. WADA
  pres. **Witold Banka**: "outrageous"; WADA "lost confidence in GADA." Disciplinary process ongoing. ✓
- **2026 Nations Cup:** Georgia in Pool B (with Romania, Zimbabwe, Portugal, Spain, HK China); crossover
  July fixtures — **Uruguay host Georgia, Montevideo 4 July**; **Chile v Georgia, Santiago** (late July).
