# Spain, Generation 3 scout: "The Colegios Mayores" (1966 - 1989/90)

Scope: generation-wide scouting pass (university clubs, federation under Noriega/Cortazar/Pico, the transition, the national side, 1987, the regions, women's beginnings, scenes). The best new material is El País's 1976-83 archive (reachable through `elpais.com/diario/YYYY/MM/DD/deportes/` day indexes) and the Arquitectura club history after 1974. Facts already in hand from the yearbook (finals, tables, caps, presidents' list) are not repeated except where a source adds to them.

## 3. The transition (1975-82)

- **fact:** The cup was the "Campeonato de S.E. el Generalísimo" in the yearbook's table, with the "Copa de S.M. el Rey" heading above the later finals, so the rename falls between the 1975-76 and 1976-77 finals.

  - quote: "Copa de S.M. el Rey"
  - status: VERIFIED

- **fact:** Spain's Consejo Superior de Deportes was established by Decree 2258/1977 of 27 August, successor to the Delegación Nacional de Deportes (founded 22 February 1941).
  - url: https://www.crwflags.com/fotw/flags/es@csd.html
  - quote: "It was established through Decree No. 2258/1977, of August 27"
  - quote: "which was established on February 22, 1941"

## Conflicts

- **Maori score, Barcelona, 17 Nov 1982:** El País (19-20 Nov 1982) gives 62-13; El País (6 Nov 1988) says Spain lost "54-6 y 66-3" six years earlier. El Independiente (2022) gives 62-13 for Barcelona, 66-3 for Madrid. FER's 2023 piece writes the Madrid score as "(3-66)". Barcelona 62-13 (two 1982 sources) is more likely than 54-6; the Madrid 66-3 has three sources.
- **Maori 1988, Seville:** El País (6 Nov 1988) says the Maoris won 22-12; a search-engine summary of another page said Spain lost "12 a 21"; I did not read that page and 22-12 is the contemporary figure.
- **Pumas venue, 23 Nov 1982:** El País says Vallehermoso; A Pleno Rugby (Argentine site) says "el 23 de noviembre de 1982, en el Campo Universitario de Madrid" (url: https://www.aplenorugby.com.ar/site/los-pumas/notas/76170/los-antecedentes-de-los-pumas-con-espana). Use El País.
- **Yearbook final numbering:** the 21-22 yearbook numbers the 1966-67 final "XXXV"; a search summary of the 2018-19 yearbook showed "XXXIV". Irrelevant to the score (14-3).
- **Arquitectura first league title:** the club history says 1974 in the text and shows a 1975 caption ("Campeón de División de Honor en 1975"); the FER list (Arquitectura 9 leagues) gives 1973-74 and 1974-75, so both are real titles.
- **Pico's surname:** FER's honours list prints "PICO MARÍN" (https://ferugby.es/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/PARTE-4-MEDALLAS-Y-RECOMPENSAS.pdf, quote "D. Alberto PICO MARIN"); Libertad Digital's obituary has "Pico Martín" in the headline URL. Use the FER spelling.
- **Zimbabwe tour:** FER says five matches in "poco más de tres semanas"; El País (2 July 1989) says four matches between 5 and 15 July. Possibly four on the main leg plus a fifth, or one source counts only the part after arrival.
- **Cisneros league years:** Madrilanea gives 1976 and 1985; FER's honours list gives 1975-76 and 1984-85 (same seasons).
- **Murrayfield 25-7:** FER's "23 momentos" gives 25-7 but no date; a programme dealer lists Scotland v Spain at Murrayfield on 19 April 1987 and another dealer calls a 1987 programme "Scotland XV". Not confirmed that these are the same match; check the yearbook's international list.
