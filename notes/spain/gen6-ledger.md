## Sequences (main thread)

- **Uruguay, continued:** Spain won in Montevideo 41–21 (2019) and 32–20 (2020) and lost there 10–19 five days later.
- **The second qualifying table on the field:** over 2021 and 2022 Spain played ten matches and won six (Russia twice, the Netherlands twice, Romania and Portugal in Madrid); the ten points deducted were the ten league points of the two Netherlands matches. Positions and totals of the combined table are not in the yearbook: scout.
- **FC Barcelona reached a cup final in 2019** and lost by a point: the first final for the club since 1985.
