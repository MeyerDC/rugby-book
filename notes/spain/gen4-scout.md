# Spain, Generation 4 scout: "Galashiels" (1990 - 1999)

Scope: generation-wide scouting pass (the 1999 World Cup match by match, the 1998 qualification, the misses of 1991 and 1995, professionalism, the clubs, the federation, landmark Tests, women). Best new material: El País day-by-day reports (1990, 1994, 1998, 1999) and the FER's 2019/2020 retrospectives with full line-ups. Scores, dates and venues already in the yearbook extract are not repeated except where a source adds to them.

## 2. How Spain qualified for 1999

- **fact:** El País (agency copy from Edinburgh, 3 Dec 1998) reported Spain beat Portugal 21-17 at Murrayfield and qualified for the first time, as the 18th of 20 teams.
  - url: https://elpais.com/diario/1998/12/03/deportes/912639610_850215.html
  - quote: "España ganó, por 21-17, a Portugal, en el gran escenario escocés de Murrayfield, y se clasificó, por primera vez"
  - quote: "España es el 18º equipo clasificado para la Copa, en la que jugarán 20 selecciones"
  - status: VERIFIED

- **fact:** In that match Serguei Kovalenko, described as stand-off "of Ukrainian origin", kicked six penalties for 18 points, Díez dropped a goal, and Spain played from minute 26 a man short after the back-row man Díaz was sent off.
  - url: https://elpais.com/diario/1998/12/03/deportes/912639610_850215.html
  - quote: "seis golpes de castigo transformados por el medio de apertura, de origen ucranio, Serguei Kovalenko (18 puntos)"
  - quote: "España jugó desde el minuto 26 con un hombre menos, el tercera línea, Díaz, expulsado."
  - status: VERIFIED

- **fact:** El País noted that even a defeat would not have ended Spain's hopes, because a play-off against Uruguay and Morocco remained.
  - url: https://elpais.com/diario/1998/12/03/deportes/912639610_850215.html
  - quote: "aún quedaba una repesca contra Uruguay y Marruecos"
  - status: VERIFIED

- **fact:** The FER describes the Portugal tie as two legs, Elche 33-22 and Murrayfield 21-17, under coach Feijoo, with José Díaz a "reinforcement of great experience" who had come from Castres and was shown the red card.
  - url: http://ferugby.es/revive-en-teledeporte-la-clasificacion-de-espana-para-el-mundial-de-1999
  - quote: "en una eliminatoria a ida y vuelta, en Elche (33-22) y Murrayfield (21-17)"
  - quote: "refuerzo de gran experiencia que había llegado de Castres"
  - quote: "los Leones, dirigidos por el actual presidente de la Federación Española de Rugby (FER), Alfonso Feijoo"
  - status: VERIFIED

- **fact:** The FER match sheet for the qualifier names Feijoo and Pardo as coaches, Steve Lander as referee, and Spain's XV with Malo at number 6 and Kovalenko at 10.
  - url: http://ferugby.es/revive-en-teledeporte-la-clasificacion-de-espana-para-el-mundial-de-1999
  - quote: "Entrenadores : Alfonso FEIJOO y Tomás PARDO"
  - quote: "6. Alberto MALO"
  - quote: "10. Andrei KOVALENKO"
  - status: VERIFIED

- **fact:** The FER says the route opened because the World Cup was enlarged from 16 to 20 teams, and that Spain had come close to 1991 and 1995.
  - url: http://ferugby.es/revive-en-teledeporte-la-clasificacion-de-espana-para-el-mundial-de-1999

## 3. The two misses

- **fact:** 1991: El País (4 Oct 1990) reported Spain lost 19-6 to Romania in the second qualifying match, lacking a goal-kicker once more, and, having already lost to Italy, had no chance of qualifying even if it beat Holland on the Sunday; the places would go to Italy and Romania.
  - url: https://elpais.com/diario/1990/10/04/deportes/654994812_850215.html
  - quote: "España perdió, por 19-6 contra Rumania, en el segundo partido de clasificación para la Copa del Mundo de 1991."
  - quote: "Volvió a carecer de un pateador en los golpes de castigo."
  - quote: "Como ya había perdido con Italia, España no tiene opción a clasificarse, aunque gane el domingo a Holanda."
  - status: VERIFIED

- **fact:** 1995: El País (22 May 1994) reported Spain lost 0-54 to Wales in Madrid in "the most important match of its history in this sport", a qualifier for the Cup in South Africa, and that Wales had beaten Portugal 116-12 and Spain would face Portugal the next Saturday.
  - url: https://elpais.com/diario/1994/05/22/deportes/769557603_850215.html
  - quote: "España perdió ayer en Madrid por goleada, 0-54, ante Gales en el partido más importante de su historia en este deporte."
  - quote: "El encuentro fue valedero para una de las plazas de la Copa del Mundo"
  - quote: "al que Gales arrolló por 12-116"
  - status: VERIFIED

- **fact:** The Rugby Journal summarises both misses as topping the initial groups and then losing, to Romania (1991) and Wales (1995).
  - url: https://www.therugbyjournal.com/shirt-stories/spain-1999
  - quote: "topping their initial groups before losing to Romania and Wales respectively"
  - status: VERIFIED

- **fact:** The FER's 1999 qualifier piece says Spain "stayed close" to both the 1991 and 1995 World Cups.
  - url: http://ferugby.es/revive-en-teledeporte-la-clasificacion-de-espana-para-el-mundial-de-1999

## 4. Professionalism (August 1995) and Spain

- **fact:** The FER's own verdict: from 1995 Spain "retreats by leaps and bounds" against the powers it had started to match, citing Australia Emerging in 1991 against a heavy loss to the Wallabies on their 2001 visit.
  - url: https://ferugby.es/que-debes-saber-de-la-historia-del-rugby-en-espana-23-momentos-en-los-101-anos-de-la-fer/
  - quote: "En 1995, cuando el rugby se convierte en profesional, España retrocede a pasos agigantados"
  - quote: "competir en 1991 ante Australia Emerging"
  - status: VERIFIED

- **fact:** El País's 7 Dec 1998 column said the Spanish federation had not evolved as it should to make the leap to professionalism, that Spanish rugby was still an amateur sport with few sponsors, and that it was "anchored in a prehistoric rugby".
  - url: https://elpais.com/diario/1998/12/07/deportes/912985222_850215.html
  - quote: "La Federación Española de Rugby, que no ha evolucionado como debiera para dar el salto al profesionalismo"
  - quote: "Sigue siendo un deporte de aficionados, con escasos patrocinadores"
  - quote: "sigue anclada en un rugby prehistórico"
  - status: VERIFIED

- **fact:** The same column reports that the federation had lost the bid to organise a Sevens World Cup to Argentina, and that Spain had conceded almost 300 points in seven matches that season against French clubs in the second division of the European Cup.
  - url: https://elpais.com/diario/1998/12/07/deportes/912985222_850215.html
  - quote: "Le falló la organización del Mundial a 7"

## 5. The clubs and the regions in the 1990s

- **fact:** Getxo: founded 1975 by three players from Gernika; early squads drawn from Getxo's traineras rowers; played at the municipal sports ground of Fadura; black and yellow colours.
  - url: https://getxorugby.eus/historia-del-getxo-rugby/
  - quote: "Fundado en 1975 por tres jugadores procedentes del Gernika Rugby Taldea"
  - quote: "especialmente de remeros de traineras"
  - quote: "desde la Ciudad Deportiva Municipal de Fadura"
  - status: VERIFIED

- **fact:** Getxo's first cup, 3 June 1990, 28-7 over Santboiana, opens the club's "golden age", followed by cups in 1991, 1992 and 1997 and the league in 1993; unbeaten in more than 45 league matches at Fadura between 1991 and 1995.
  - url: https://getxorugby.eus/historia-del-getxo-rugby/
  - quote: "Los años 90 marcaron la época dorada."
  - quote: "El primer gran hito llegó el 3 de junio de 1990 con la conquista de la Copa del Rey tras vencer a la Santboiana (28-7)."
  - quote: "Entre 1991 y 1995 el equipo permaneció imbatido en Fadura durante más de 45 partidos ligueros."
  - status: VERIFIED

- **fact:** Getxo's palmarés gives the 1993 league final (4 April 1993) as a two-leg win over Liceo Francés, 40-8 on the day (71-21 on aggregate), and the 1993 cup final as a 6-13 loss to El Salvador, 13 June 1993.
  - url: https://getxorugby.eus/palmares-del-getxo-rugby-2/
  - quote: "1993-04-04 Final de División de Honor Getxo Rugby vs Liceo Francés | 40-8 (Global: 71-21)"
  - quote: "1993-06-13 Final de Copa del Rey Getxo Rugby vs El Salvador | 6-13"
  - status: VERIFIED

- **fact:** The same palmarés gives Getxo's 1991 final as 32-15 over Gernika (9 June 1991), 1992 as 20-9 over Mercabarna-BUC (3 May 1992), a 1995 final lost to El Monte 25-37, and the 1997 final won 37-19 against Cajasol Ciencias.
  - url: https://getxorugby.eus/palmares-del-getxo-rugby-2/
  - quote: "1991-06-09 Final de Copa del Rey Getxo Rugby vs Gernika RT | 32-15"

  - status: VERIFIED

- **fact:** Ciencias (Seville): founded 1972 in the Faculty of Sciences of the University of Seville, detached from the Faculty in 1977; two league titles (1992, 1994), three cups (1994, 1995, 1996) and an Ibérica (1995) in the 1990s; training at La Cartuja in 2012.
  - url: https://blogs.canalsur.es/documentacionyarchivo/del-club-de-rugby-ciencias-a-ciencias-fundacion-cajasol-sevilla/

- **fact:** Arquitectura's last two titles of the decade (1990 and 1995) came under captains Loscertales and Hernández-Gil, with Laurie O'Reilly as coach for the first; the club's own history says Seville's Ciencias and Valladolid's Quesos displaced Madrid's classics Cisneros and Canoe, and Getxo was the great new rival.

  - url: https://vracrugby.com/club/historia-palmares/
