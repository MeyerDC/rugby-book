## 2. The May 2018 eligibility ruling

- **fact:** World Rugby, 15 May 2018: the independent committee found Belgium (7 occasions), Romania (8) and Spain (9, of which 8 in Rugby World Cup 2019 qualifying) fielded ineligible players in the 2017 and 2018 Rugby Europe Championships.
  - url: https://www.world.rugby/news/334795/2019?lang=en
  - quote: "Spain had fielded one or more ineligible players on 9 occasions during the 2017 and 2018 Rugby Europe Championships (of which 8 matches related to Rugby World Cup 2019 qualifying)"
  - quote: "Romania has fielded one ineligible player on 8 occasions"
  - status: VERIFIED

- **fact:** Sanctions: a 5-point deduction per match (40 for Spain, 30 each for Belgium and Romania), so Russia replaced Romania as Europe 1 and Germany replaced Spain in the play-off against Portugal; suspended fines of GBP 125,000 (Belgium), 50,000 (Spain), 100,000 (Romania).
  - url: https://www.world.rugby/news/334795/2019?lang=en
  - quote: "The deduction of 5 points for any match in which a union fielded an ineligible player (40-point deduction for Spain, and a 30-point deduction for both Belgium and Romania)"
  - quote: "Russia would qualify as Europe 1 into Pool A replacing Romania and Germany will replace Spain in the European play-off against Portugal"
  - quote: "Spain: £50,000 GBP (at a rate of £25,000 for a union not on Council x 2 ineligible players)"
  - status: VERIFIED

- **fact:** The committee's finding on good faith, in its own words.
  - url: https://www.world.rugby/news/334795/2019?lang=en
  - quote: "they had not acted in bad faith"
  - quote: "it was desirable to take any steps which avoided the risks of qualification being determined off the pitch"
  - status: VERIFIED

- **fact:** The two Spanish players were Mathieu Belie and Bastien Fuster, previously capped by France Under-20s; Romania's one was Sione Faka'osilea (ex Tonga Sevens). (SBS/AFP-style report, 15 May 2018)

  - url: https://www.sbs.com.au/news/article/russia-qualify-for-rugby-world-cup-after-romania-belgium-and-spain-are-penalised/fo1b2ftjf

## 4. The April 2022 case

- **fact:** 28 April 2022: the independent judicial committee (Nigel Hampton QC chair, Pamela Woodman, Frank Hadden) found Spain fielded an ineligible player in two RWC 2023 qualifying matches; 5 points deducted per match (10), GBP 25,000 fixed fine, and the suspended GBP 50,000 from 2018 payable.
  - url: https://www.rugbyworldcup.com/fr/news/710182/independent-judicial-committee-decision-rugby-world-cup-2023-european-qualifying
