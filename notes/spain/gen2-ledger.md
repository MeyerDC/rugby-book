# Spain, Generation 2 — the ledger (1936–1966)

Generation-wide research, written by hand in the main thread on 10 October 2026 from the federation's own yearbook (Anuario 2021-22) and its "23 momentos" page, before the subplots are organised. It holds only what the federation's records say: presidents, every cup final, the two league seasons, every senior international. Everything the ledgers cannot say (the war, the S.E.U., the regime's apparatus, the Catalan federation, the colleges) is in `gen2-scout.md`. No chapter prose.

**Read the counts at the end before planning.** They change the Gate 1 proposal: the field was more Catalan after the war than before it.

## The league that lasted two seasons

- **fact:** A national league, the *Copa Presidente del Gobierno*, was played in 1952–53 and 1953–54. FC Barcelona won both, with Atlético de Madrid second both times. The S.E.U. de Madrid side appears in the second season's table under the name "Mayo Madrid". The yearbook's next league season is 1969–70.

## Counts (main thread's arithmetic from the entries above; recount at S3)

**Cup finals, 1940–41 to 1964–65: 25.**
- **Barcelona-area clubs won 20:** FC Barcelona 11 (1941–42, 1943–46, 1949–53, 1954–56, 1964–65), Santboiana 7 (1942–43, 1947–48, 1957–62), Natació Barcelona 2 (1956–57, 1962–63).
- **Madrid clubs won 5:** S.E.U. de Madrid 3 (1940–41, 1946–47, 1953–54), Atlético de Madrid 1 (1948–49), Canoe 1 (1963–64).
- **S.E.U. sides reached 9 finals** (Madrid 8, León 1) and won 3. The last was 1953–54. After that the student union is absent from the final for the rest of the generation.
- **From 1954–55 to 1962–63, nine straight titles went to Barcelona-area clubs**, and at least six of those finals were between two of them (1955–56, 1956–57, 1957–58, 1959–60, 1960–61, 1962–63; Picadero Juventud in 1958–59 is to be placed by the scout).
- Before the war Catalan clubs had won 6 of 8 cups. After it they won 20 of 25.

**Senior men's internationals, 6 May 1951 to 1 May 1965: 25.** Won 7 (Belgium 1954, 1958, 1959 and 1960; Portugal 1954 and 1965; West Germany 1960), drawn 3 (West Germany 1954; RAF 1953 and 1955), lost 15. Four of the 25 are against the RAF (1953, 1955, 1962, 1965), a service side, which the ledger lists with the internationals. (An earlier version of this line said three; corrected at S3.)
- **Fifteen at home:** Barcelona 9, Madrid 4, Valencia 1, Seville 1. The national side played more than twice as often in Barcelona as in Madrid.
- **Ten away or neutral:** Rome, Frankfurt, Treviso, Naples, Limoges, Liège, Charleroi, Brussels, Heidelberg, Lisbon.
- Never beat Italy, France or Romania in this generation; no win over anyone before April 1954.

## Corrections to the Gate 1 proposal of 10 October

- **The Italy matches of 17 February 1963 and 1 March 1964 are Sub-23**, not senior (the ledger lists them under "Sub-23" in the Italy section). The scout agent's brief listed them as Tests; they are not.
- **"27 marzo 1966 España Portugal 0-3"** in the Portugal section is the **Júnior** match of the same day, not a conflicting senior score.
- **The league did not "begin in the early 1950s" and continue.** It ran two seasons and stopped for fifteen years.
- **The plan's answer "it left Barcelona's clubs on the field" understates it.** The record says the regime's student union won the first cup and was then beaten by FC Barcelona four finals in five.
