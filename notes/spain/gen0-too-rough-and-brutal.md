## The scene: Bonanova, February 1901

- **fact:** In February 1901, at the Bonanova velodrome, FC Barcelona and Català FC players also tried out rugby (reported by "Un Delantero", 1901, p. 86).
  - url: https://dialnet.unirioja.es/descarga/articulo/7666468.pdf
  - quote: "Así sucedió en febrero de 1901 cuando en el velódromo de la Bonanova jugadores del FC Barcelona y del Català FC"
  - quote: "Efectuáronse asimismo en dicho terreno algunos ensayos de foot-ball Rugby"
  - status: VERIFIED (secondary, quoting the period press)
- **fact:** A 1902 profile of FC Barcelona's first and second teams lists the rugby behind them: Gamper at Lyon, Arthur Witty at school in Liverpool from the age of 11 (first XV 1893–95), Alfonso Albéniz at Racing Club de Paris, Jaime Alberto Eckes at school in Liverpool (1893), Cyril Alexander in Scotland ("rugby only").
  - url: https://dialnet.unirioja.es/descarga/articulo/7666468.pdf
  - quote: "Entre estos jóvenes se encontraban algunos de los jugadores del primer y segundo equipo del FC Barcelona"
  - quote: "Alfonso Albéniz “miembro del “Racing Club” de París en donde ha practicado el foot-ball rugby”"
  - quote: "Cyril Alexander que había “practicado en Escocia el rugby solamente”"
  - status: VERIFIED (secondary)

## FC Barcelona's rugby inheritance

- **fact:** Gamper played for Union Athlétique de Lyon, the local rugby club, the year before he founded FC Barcelona; the period paper *Lyon Sport* rated him among the region's best.
  - url: https://www.fcbarcelona.com/en/news/1055136/barca-founder-gampers-year-in-lyon
  - quote: "He joined Union Athlétique de Lyon , the local rugby club, and was soon excelling at the unfamiliar code"
  - quote: "Although he had only played association football … he quickly learned the rules of rugby football, contributing to our first team his excellent dribbling qualities. His kicks and dribbling have placed him among the top players in the region."
  - status: VERIFIED
- **fact:** The French rugby union of the day banned anyone who played association football from rugby for a year, which is the conflict Gamper stepped into at Lyon.
  - url: https://www.fcbarcelona.com/en/news/1055136/barca-founder-gampers-year-in-lyon
  - quote: "had a law stating that anybody who played it would be banned from rugby for a year"
  - status: VERIFIED — ⚠️ the club's paraphrase; confirm the rule's wording before using it as fact
- **fact:** FC Barcelona supports, without certainty, the theory that its blue-and-maroon colours came from the rugby kit of Merchant Taylors' School, Crosby, which the Witty brothers attended. The school confirmed that blue and maroon was its rugby strip in the late nineteenth century.
  - url: https://www.fcbarcelona.com/en/news/813829/the-origins-of-fc-barcelonas-colours
  - quote: "the club supports the theory that indicates Barça's colours come from the rugby team of a school by the name of Merchant Taylors in Crosby (Liverpool) which the Witty brothers attended"
  - quote: "the blue and maroon was the strip of the school’s rugby team in the late nineteenth century, in which the two brothers had competed in while doing their studies"
  - status: VERIFIED — a theory the club states as such; write it as one ("the club itself believes")
- **fact:** Gamper was a general sportsman: athletics, cycling, rugby and golf as well as football.
  - url: https://www.fcbarcelona.com/en/card/643865/1899-1909-foundation-and-survival
  - quote: "aside from football, he participated in athletics, cycling, rugby and golf"
  - status: VERIFIED

## "Not for our temperament": the press, 1894–1911

- **fact:** 1894: a Spanish gymnastics treatise (García Fraguas) already described and illustrated the game's rules.
  - url: https://dialnet.unirioja.es/descarga/articulo/7666468.pdf
  - quote: "En 1894 el profesor de Gimnástica José Esteban García Fraguas"
  - status: VERIFIED (secondary)
- **fact:** 1903: talk of two Toulouse sides coming to play at the Hipódromo, and of the Barcelona, Español and Hispania clubs raising a team against them; and a hostile verdict in the same year that the game should not be acclimatised.
  - url: https://dialnet.unirioja.es/descarga/articulo/7666468.pdf
  - quote: "Háblase de la próxima venida a esta ciudad de dos bandos de jugadores de Toulouse para jugar un partido a foot-ball Rugby en el Hipódromo"
  - quote: "ni por nuestro temperamento ni por las condiciones higiénicas de ese juego, no creemos que convenga que aquí se juegue mucho ni se trate de aclimatar el tal Rugby"
  - status: VERIFIED (secondary; Corredisses, 1903 and Miscelánea, 1903)
- **fact:** 1904–05: the Spanish press reported the casualties of the American game, and the two games were not always told apart.
  - url: https://dialnet.unirioja.es/descarga/articulo/7666468.pdf
  - quote: "un estudiante perdió la razón a cons ecuencia de los golpes recibidos"
  - status: VERIFIED (secondary; Víctimas del foot-ball, 1904) — note the PDF's broken "cons ecuencia"
- **fact:** 1904: a defender. Brutal, but better for the young than the café, the gambling table and the bullring.
  - url: https://dialnet.unirioja.es/descarga/articulo/7666468.pdf
  - quote: "Creo que es preferible esta distracción a pasar los días festivos en el café, en la timba, en los toros o de juerga"
  - status: VERIFIED (secondary; Huso, 1904, p. 128)
- **fact:** 1910: Barcelona's city council planned a match between two French sides for the Mercè festival, "for the first time in Spain"; it did not take place, for reasons unknown.
  - url: https://dialnet.unirioja.es/descarga/articulo/7666468.pdf
  - quote: "por primera vez en España este emocionante y sugestivo juego"
  - quote: "no se pudo realizar por causas que desconocemos"
  - status: VERIFIED (secondary; Fiestas de Barcelona, 1910)
- **fact:** 1910, Vigo: two teams from the English squadron played at the Real Club Fortuna ground, the first match in the region; the small crowd disliked it.
  - url: https://dialnet.unirioja.es/descarga/articulo/7666468.pdf
  - quote: "Al público escaso que presenció la lucha no le gustó el nuevo deporte, por lo poco elegante y muy violento"
  - quote: "El partido sólo sirvió para admirar la serenidad y la sangre fría de los hijos de la Gran Bretaña"
  - status: VERIFIED (secondary; Gonzalito, 1910, p. 1)
- **fact:** 12–13 February 1911, Monelos park, A Coruña: officers of the ships *Liverpool* and *Gloucester*, which Torrebadella places in the Royal Navy, played rugby after football matches against Real Club Deportivo; the verdict. The news reached Barcelona, where a planned rugby team drew the same objection.
  - url: https://dialnet.unirioja.es/descarga/articulo/7666468.pdf
  - quote: "En La Coruña, otros encuentros tenían como protagonista a la Royal Navy"
  - quote: "los días 12 y 13 de febrero de 1911 confraternizaron un match de rugby en el parque de Monelos"
  - quote: "Resulta demasiado fuerte y brutal"
  - quote: "zancadillas, trastazos y demás barbaridades"
  - status: VERIFIED (secondary; Los partidos de ayer, 1911; Rofi, 1911)
