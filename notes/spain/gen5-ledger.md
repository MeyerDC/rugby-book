# Spain, Generation 5 — the ledger (2000–2018)

Generation-wide research, by hand in the main thread, 11 October 2026, from the federation's yearbook (Anuario 2021-22). Presidents, every cup final, league champions, every European championship season, World Cup qualifiers and other senior men's internationals. Quotes are the yearbook's own lines. No chapter prose.

## Presidents

- **fact:** After Pico (Madrid), the presidency went to Alfonso Mandado Vázquez of Pontevedra in 2001 (with an acting president from San Sebastián listed the same year), Javier González Cancho of Valladolid in 2012, and Alfonso Feijoo García of San Sebastián in 2014. No president of this generation is from Madrid or Barcelona.

## European championship, 2000–2018 (Spain's lines and season summaries)

- **fact:** Spain in the European Nations Cup (the Rugby Europe Championship from 2017): fourth in 2000 and 2001, fifth in 2002, sixth and relegated in 2003–04; first in the second division in 2004–06 and promoted; fourth in 2007–08; fifth in 2008–09, 2010 and 2011; second in 2012, with wins over Georgia (25–18) and Romania (13–12) in Madrid; sixth in 2013; fourth in 2014; third in 2015; fourth in 2016; third in 2017. In 2018: won in Russia (20–13, 10 February), beat Romania 22–10 in Madrid (18 February) and Germany 84–10 (11 March), and lost 18–10 to Belgium in Brussels on 18 March.
