## 3 June 1973: Spain 23–30 France, la Foixarda, Barcelona

- **fact:** *La Vanguardia* of 5 June 1973, page 42, headed its report 'RUGBY: EN EL CAMPO DE LA FUXARDA… Francia venció a España por 30 a 23, en encuentro para la Copa de la F.I.R.A.' The match was at the Foixarda ground on Montjuïc, in the morning ('la mañana cálida de Montjuich'), before a full ground: 'a brilliant and unusual spectacle of a numerous public'.

  - quote: "que hizo subir la emoción en la mañana"
  - quote: "cálida de Montjuich. El resultado final del"
  - status: VERIFIED
  - note: the federation's ledger has the same match: 3 junio 1973 España Francia en Barcelona 23-30, in Spain's first season in FIRA's top group.

- **fact:** The reporter, J. Pardo, read the result as a turning point: the match 'has begun to mark a brilliant recovery of Spanish rugby'; until then Spanish rugby 'was considered internationally third-rate', but from this match, 'to the great surprise of many, the French included', Spain began to count among the sides to be reckoned with in Europe. France, the favourites for the tie and the cup, were 'very disconcerted' and had to send on their best players; Spain drew level at one point; a bigger margin had been expected, France being used to much wider wins over Spain.

  - quote: "con gran sorpresa para muchos, incluidos"
  - quote: "victorias mucho más amplias en sus ante-"

  - status: VERIFIED

- **fact:** The referee was an Italian; Spain's fifteen as printed: García Adolfo, Santana, Corujo, Feijoo, Del Rey, Moriche, Luque, Epalza, Bueno, Lázaro, Ángel García, Blanco, Mocoroa, Zapiain and Verdugo. A **Feijoo** is in the side (an Alfonso Feijoo García of San Sebastián became FER president in 2014: same man not established here) and a **Moriche** (Arquitectura's history names Manuel Moriche as its outstanding player of the 1970s).

## Arquitectura's own history, 1970–1988 (read directly)

- **fact:** In 1970 Arquitectura took legal form 'at the request of the Spanish Rugby Federation', as the Club Deportivo Arquitectura de Madrid, and was promoted that season; it won its first national league in 1974, coached by Carmelo García 'Margarito' and captained by Andrés Zulet.
  - url: https://www.arquitectura-rugby.org/club/historia-club-deportivo-arquitectura/
  - quote: "Arquitectura toma forma jurídica a petición de la Federación Española de Rugby"
  - quote: "bajo le batuta de"
  - status: VERIFIED

- **fact:** The club names its internationals of the 1970s, Moriche among them, and quotes the Welsh coach Ray Williams's view that Manuel Moriche 'could without doubt have been first-choice fly-half of any of the Five Nations sides of the time'.
  - url: https://www.arquitectura-rugby.org/club/historia-club-deportivo-arquitectura/
  - quote: "por citar sólo a los internacionales de esa década"
  - quote: "podría haber sido sin duda, apertura titular de cualquier equipo de las cinco naciones de la época"
  - status: VERIFIED

- **fact:** The Welsh connection: in London in 1974 the club met 'a young Ray Williams' who agreed to coach them that summer; after his training camp ('stage') at Elizondo in June 1975 the club sent many of its coaches to the Welsh Rugby Union's summer courses. It toured Britain in 1974, 1976 and 1977, beating Camberley, London Scottish and London Welsh on the last tour.
  - url: https://www.arquitectura-rugby.org/club/historia-club-deportivo-arquitectura/
  - quote: "de Ray Williams en Elizondo en junio de 1975, se trabajó duro en la preparación de entrenadores, enviando a muchos de ellos a los cursos que impartía en verano la Welsh Rugby Union"
  - quote: "Volvemos al Reino Unido en 1976 y 1977, regresando de la última gira con históricas victorias conrra"
  - status: VERIFIED

- **fact:** By 1976 the club ran twelve rugby teams a week, 'including a women's team that made its debut in 1976', captained by María Arenillas.
  - url: https://www.arquitectura-rugby.org/club/historia-club-deportivo-arquitectura/
  - quote: "En 1976 Arquitectura disponía de doce equipos de Rugby compitiendo semanalmente, incluyendo un equipo femenino que debuto en 1976 capitaneado por"
  - status: VERIFIED

- **fact:** In 1977 the Consejo Superior de Deportes gave the club the Copa Stadium as the best sports club in the country, and the trophy was received from the hands of King Juan Carlos; in 1981 the King accepted the honorary presidency of the club's fiftieth-anniversary events.
  - url: https://www.arquitectura-rugby.org/club/historia-club-deportivo-arquitectura/
  - quote: "Todo este esfuerzo ejemplar fue reconocido en 1977 por el Consejo superior de Deportes con la"
  - quote: "S.M. el Rey Don Juan Carlos."
  - quote: "presidencia de honor de SM el Rey Don Juan Carlos"
  - status: VERIFIED

- **fact:** In May 1979 the club played Northern Transvaal, of South Africa, at the Vallehermoso stadium in Madrid, and in the 1980s it toured South Africa, playing the South African Defence Force and Stellenbosch University.
  - url: https://www.arquitectura-rugby.org/club/historia-club-deportivo-arquitectura/
  - quote: "mayo de 1979 recibimos en el estadio Vallehermoso a"
  - quote: "Universidad de Stellenbosch"
  - status: VERIFIED
  - note: these are club fixtures with apartheid-era South African sides, in the years of the sporting boycott told in the South Africa chapter. The club history reports them without comment. Use plainly, if at all; do not characterise.

- **fact:** The club's rivals of the decade: 'Cisneros, Canoe and CAU, direct rivals at the start of the decade and later in the regional group competitions that preceded the División de Honor finals'. Its 1980s honours as it lists them: leagues of 1981, 1982, 1986 and 1988, King's Cups of 1980, 1981, 1984 and 1988.
  - url: https://www.arquitectura-rugby.org/club/historia-club-deportivo-arquitectura/

## The federation's own narrative of the 1980s

- **fact:** The FER's '23 momentos' makes the 1989 tour of Zimbabwe its marker for the decade: Spanish rugby 'had been in full development, reaching a peak' in a tour of five matches in little more than three weeks, three against regional sides and two Tests, both won, at Bulawayo and Harare (the page calls the beaten Zimbabweans 'los sudafricanos', an error of its own). It lists the decade's other 'honourable feats': 'falling with dignity at Murrayfield, 25–7 to Scotland', and playing 'on level terms' against England (A or B) and the Māori.
  - url: https://ferugby.es/que-debes-saber-de-la-historia-del-rugby-en-espana-23-momentos-en-los-101-anos-de-la-fer/
  - quote: "una gira de cinco partidos de Los Leones por Zimbabue en 1989"
  - quote: "los españoles derrotaron a los sudafricanos"
  - quote: "caer con dignidad en Murrayfield por un 25-7 ante Escocia"
  - quote: "jugar de tú a tú frente a Inglaterra (A o B, pero con grandes jugadores) y Maoríes"
  - quote: "El rugby en España había estado en pleno proceso de desarrollo alcanzando un pico"
  - status: VERIFIED
  - note: the ledger dates Murrayfield to 19 April 1987 and the Zimbabwe Tests to 8 and 15 July 1989 (28–16, 14–9). 'On level terms' is the federation's phrase; the ledger has England A winning 31–6 and 31–9 and the Māori 66–3 and 22–12. Do not repeat 'sudafricanos'.

## Added by hand after the scout: the El País and El Independiente pieces read in full

- **fact:** El País, Tuesday 31 May 1977, 'El CAU, justo vencedor de Copa, en Rugby': CAU won 'la Copa del Rey' by beating Cisneros 26–6. Cisneros scored first, a try scored and converted by the full-back Perote 'in a very fast move' that caught CAU too far up; CAU's first four points were a try by 'the international Cristín', unconverted; an Apraiz penalty put CAU one point up at half-time. In the second half 'the greater strength and pressure of CAU's tireless forwards' decided it: a try by the fly-half Luque II ('Zape'), an Apraiz penalty, a try from a scrum converted by Apraiz, and a last try by Manero, converted. Referee: Imízcoz. Both line-ups printed. Cisneros's includes Mandado, Cafrune, a Noriega, a Feijoó and a Florencio; CAU's includes Cristín, Santana and Luque. The report does not give the ground or the day (the scout says the previous Sunday, which would be 29 May; the article itself does not).
  - url: https://elpais.com/diario/1977/05/31/deportes/233877625_850215.html
  - quote: "que consiguió y transformó el zaguero Perote en una jugada rapidísima"
  - quote: "dejó el marcador un punto arriba para el CAU cuando se llegó al descanso"
  - quote: "la mayor fuerza y presión de la incansable delantera del CAU terminó por inclinar el marcador"
  - quote: "Por último ensayo de Manero y nueva patada de Apraiz que dejó el marcador en el definitivo 26-6"
  - quote: "Noriega, Feijoó, Cano"

All by surname only: leads, not identities. Santana and Luque are also in the Spanish fifteen of 3 June 1973.

Semi-finals: Arquitectura v CAU, Cisneros v Canoe. The piece expected an Arquitectura v Cisneros final; CAU won it.
