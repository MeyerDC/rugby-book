# Spain, Generation 1 — Subplot 1: "The Settlement" (1928)

Research for the January 1928 FER merger and the 1928 Spain–France match at Bordeaux, per the brief.
Format follows SUMMARY.md §7.

## The January 1928 merger

- **fact:** In January 1928 a new Federación Española de Rugby was constituted in Madrid, merging the
  directorates of the two rival bodies (the breakaway RUE and the old FER), with Enrique Gutiérrez as
  president.
  - url: https://revista-ebalonmano.unex.es/index.php/ebalonmano/article/download/2090/2162/7862
  - quote: "provocó la creación en Madrid – enero de 1928– de una nueva federación española que, finalmente fusionó las directivas en disputa –de la RUE y de la FER"
  - status: VERIFIED

- **fact:** The FER's own yearbook confirms the same appointment independently: Enrique Gutiérrez, of
  Madrid, is listed as FER president from 1928, sitting between Baldiri Aleu's two terms (1923 and 1929).
  - url: https://ferugby.es/Descargar/1771/00-presidentes-y-afiliacion-internacional/87282/estructura-fer-anuario-ferugby-2021-22-parte-0.pdf
  - quote: "D. Enrique GUTIÉRREZ Madrid (1928)"
  - status: VERIFIED

- **fact:** The merger was sealed on the pitch: a reconciliation fixture for the "Copa Lord Derward"
  between Castilian and Catalan representative sides at Madrid's Estadio Metropolitano, won 17–0 by the
  Catalans.
  - url: https://revista-ebalonmano.unex.es/index.php/ebalonmano/article/download/2090/2162/7862
  - quote: "La concordia se selló dando lugar a un encuentro en el estadio Metropolitano –Copa Lord Derward– entre las selecciones Castellana y Catalana, con un resultado (0-17) a favor de los catalanes"
  - status: VERIFIED

- **fact:** On 21 March 1928 (19 March in the article's own table) a Catalan selection beat a Centro
  selection 17–0 in Madrid, a match Torrebadella calls especially significant because it sealed "a
  first schism" in the federative disputes. *(Main-thread review: the agent called this a fixture
  distinct from the Lord Derward match above. Nothing in the quotes supports that: same teams, same city,
  same score, and Ordóñez's 5 April headline is this result. Treat them as probably one match, the
  reconciliation, and confirm at S2 by reading Torrebadella's passage in full.)*
  - url: https://revista-ebalonmano.unex.es/index.php/ebalonmano/article/download/2090/2162/7862
  - quote: "El 21 de marzo de 1928 se disputó en Madrid un primer partido interregional entre la Selección Catalana y la Selección de Centro; el resultado fue favorable a los catalanes (17- 0). Como se verá más adelante, este partido fue especialmente significativo, puesto que selló un primer cisma entre las disputas federativas castellanas y catalanas"
  - status: VERIFIED (see Conflicts: the article's own summary table dates this 19/03/1928, not 21 March)

- **fact:** The federative split delayed the national club championship; the Campeonato de España did not

## A contemporary/scholarly voice

- **fact:** Torrebadella's own conclusion frames the period as one in which rugby's development was
  mediated by a centralizing, repressive political climate hostile to Catalan sporting nationalism.
  - url: https://revista-ebalonmano.unex.es/index.php/ebalonmano/article/download/2090/2162/7862
  - quote: "Se concluye que el desarrollo del rugby estuvo mediado en un difícil contexto político centralizador y represivo, que conformó un ambiente hostil al nacionalismo deportivo catalán."
  - status: VERIFIED

- **fact:** His closing paragraph states plainly that the Barcelona–Madrid rivalry lay beyond sport
  itself, reflecting incompatible nationalist sentiments, with the Primo de Rivera dictatorship favouring
  Castilian rugby over Catalan.
  - url: https://revista-ebalonmano.unex.es/index.php/ebalonmano/article/download/2090/2162/7862
  - quote: "la dictadura de Primo obstaculizó los intereses del rugby catalán y apoyó al rugby castellano"

## Conflicts

- **The Bordeaux match's date — three different answers, two of them from the FER itself.**
  - Torrebadella (2024), citing a period press preview (Heraldo Deportivo, 5 April 1928) and his own
    summary table: **8 April 1928**.
  - The FER's own Anuario, in a *separate* document (the historical player register, "Histórico
    Jugadores"), independently agrees: **8 April 1928**, for all thirteen named debutants.
  - The FER's own Anuario, in yet another document (the "Historial de Competiciones Internacionales"
    ledger), gives **8 March 1928** — a full month off, and with the score reversed in emphasis
    (53–6 rather than 5–53).
  - The FER's own public-facing history ("23 momentos en los 101 años de la FER") gives **19 April
    1928** and calls this Spain's "primer partido oficial."
  - **Settled by the match-day press:** *La Petite Gironde*, Monday 9 April 1928, reports the match as
    played the day before, "par cette tiède après-midi de Pâques". **8 April 1928** is now three sources
    (press, Torrebadella, the FER register) against two FER dates that don't agree with each other.
  - Weight of evidence before the press was found: two independent sources (Torrebadella's press citation and the FER's own player
    register) converge on 8 April 1928, against two other FER-published dates that disagree with each
    other as much as with that consensus. The brief noted 8 March and 19 April are both Thursdays; 8
    April 1928 was a Sunday — the normal day for these early representative matches (the confirmed 26 May
