# Spain, Generation 1 — Subplot 2: “The Exposition Match” (1929–1930)

S1 research dossier. Quotations below are exact source text; English glosses appear only outside quotations. No chapter prose is drafted here.

## Barcelona, 1929: date, score, sequence, and venue

The Fundació Barcelona Olímpica's 2012 archival booklet transcribes *La Vanguardia* of Tuesday 21 May 1929, p. 23. The booklet's sports-programme list and this next-day account place the rugby match on **Monday 20 May**, at the inauguration of the Estadi de Montjuïc. This resolves the yearbook's 19 May entry in favour of the contemporary press. The 19 May date belongs to the opening of the Exposition itself (see below).

- **fact:** The Spain–Italy rugby match was held on 20 May 1929 as the first event at the newly inaugurated Montjuïc stadium, with Spain winning 9–0.
  - url: https://www.fundaciobarcelonaolimpica.es/wp-content/uploads/sites/3/2019/07/AH_1_expo-universal-1929_WEB.pdf
  - quote: "20/05/29 | Espanya-Itàlia (inauguració estadi)"
  - status: VERIFIED

- **fact:** *La Vanguardia*'s 21 May 1929 report explicitly identifies the ground as the Estadio de Montjuïc and the score as nine to nil before a crowd it estimates at more than 60,000, with the Spanish royal family present.
  - url: https://www.fundaciobarcelonaolimpica.es/wp-content/uploads/sites/3/2019/07/AH_1_expo-universal-1929_WEB.pdf
  - quote: "La inauguración del Estadio de Montjuïch constituye un éxito resonante."
  - quote: "Con asistencia de los Reyes, y de más de 60.000 personas, se inaugura el Estadio, venciendo nuestras selecciones, en rugby, a los italianos por nueve a cero"
  - status: VERIFIED

- **fact:** The rugby match opened the stadium programme, before the Catalan football selection met Bolton Wanderers.
  - url: https://www.fundaciobarcelonaolimpica.es/wp-content/uploads/sites/3/2019/07/AH_1_expo-universal-1929_WEB.pdf
  - quote: "Con el partido de Rugby se inauguró el Estadio"
  - status: VERIFIED

- **fact:** The FER 2021–22 yearbook's Italy head-to-head ledger gives **19 May 1929** for the same 9–0 match in Barcelona, with the competition described as the Exposición Internacional.
  - url: https://ferugby.es/Descargar/1045/05-palmares/86559/anuario-21-22-parte-4-historial-competiciones-internacionales.pdf
  - quote: "19 mayo 1929 España Italia 9-0 Barcelona (Exposición Internacional)"
  - status: VERIFIED

## The first home Test and first win: FER ledger audit

- **fact:** The FER's historical senior men's France ledger records a 6–66 loss at Madrid in 1927 and a 53–6 loss at Bordeaux in 1928. The 1927 home match was unsanctioned by the reunited FER (see *The Settlement* dossier for the sanction distinction); thus Barcelona 1929 was the first **sanctioned** home match, subject to a complete chronological audit below.
  - url: https://ferugby.es/Descargar/1045/05-palmares/86559/anuario-21-22-parte-4-historial-competiciones-internacionales.pdf
  - quote: "1 marzo 1927 España Francia 6-66 Madrid (Amistoso)"
  - quote: "8 marzo 1928 Francia España 53-6 Burdeos (Amistoso)"
  - status: VERIFIED

Commentary: the FER ledger contains known erroneous dates for both of these France matches; the 1927 match was 26 May, and the 1928 match 8 April, as established from contemporary press in the preceding dossier. Its score and venue entries are used here only to identify the fixtures, not to validate the dates.

## Aleu, Vaccaro, and the Exposition

- **fact:** The archival booklet places the match among the Exposition's sporting programme, so this was an Exposition event, not merely a fixture coincidentally held in Barcelona during it.
  - url: https://www.fundaciobarcelonaolimpica.es/wp-content/uploads/sites/3/2019/07/AH_1_expo-universal-1929_WEB.pdf

## Contemporary voice

- **fact:** The contemporary *La Vanguardia* account gives a ceremonial opening: the Italian players carried a Spanish flag, the Spanish players an Italian one, followed by anthems and an exchange of flowers.
  - url: https://www.fundaciobarcelonaolimpica.es/wp-content/uploads/sites/3/2019/07/AH_1_expo-universal-1929_WEB.pdf
  - quote: "primero apareció el equipo italiano, sosteniendo una gran bandera española; tras de ellos los españoles, con la bandera italiana."
  - quote: "La Banda Municipal interpretó en aquel momento los himnos nacionales de España y de Italia, cambiándose seguidamente ramos de flores."
  - status: VERIFIED

- **fact:** The same match report contrasts Italian physical weight with Spanish speed and skill, a contemporary game observation suitable for the scene.
  - url: https://www.fundaciobarcelonaolimpica.es/wp-content/uploads/sites/3/2019/07/AH_1_expo-universal-1929_WEB.pdf
  - quote: "los italianos más atléticos que los nuestros se impusieron por «pe-so», en cambio los de casa se defendieron y dominaron por su mayor habilidad, rapidez y conocimiento de juego."
  - status: VERIFIED

- **fact:** The Spanish monarchs arrived about fifteen minutes into the rugby game, and play paused for the royal anthem.
  - url: https://www.fundaciobarcelonaolimpica.es/wp-content/uploads/sites/3/2019/07/AH_1_expo-universal-1929_WEB.pdf

## Spain–Italy head-to-head order

  - url: https://www.federugby.it/storias/

  - quote: "29 maggio 1930, Secondo test-match per la Nazionale, che a Milano centra la prima vittoria della propria storia battendo 3-0 la Spagna."
  - status: VERIFIED

Commentary: FIR's older head-to-head page is dated 15 July 2009 and ends its match list in 2002; its headline of 27 matches and three Spanish wins is a historical snapshot, **not a current all-time count**.

## Conflicts and unresolved questions

- **Date — resolved for narrative use:** The FER ledger and BUC club history say 19 May 1929; the Exposition programme, next-day *La Vanguardia* report, and FIR say 20 May. Use **20 May** for the match, while preserving 19 May as the conflicting ledger date. The Exposition itself opened on 19 May, a plausible source of confusion, but the cause of FER's error is not proved.

- **First sanctioned home Test / first win — supported with a terminology caveat:** The FER's senior-men's opponent ledger lists only the 1927 Madrid loss to France and the 1928 Bordeaux loss to France before Italy 1929; it lists the next home fixture, Germany, on 9 June 1929. FER's own history excludes the 1927 Madrid side from its approved national-team chronology. Thus the Italy match is the first **FER-sanctioned home senior international** and first win shown by that chronology; there is no intervening home Test between May 1927 and May 1929 in the complete opponent ledger. “Test” may imply a narrower reciprocal-cap standard than FER's “encuentros internacionales”, so do not silently equate the two.
  - url: https://ferugby.es/que-debes-saber-de-la-historia-del-rugby-en-espana-23-momentos-en-los-101-anos-de-la-fer/
  - quote: "En mayo de 1927, la Rugby Unión Española había realizado otro encuentro ante la selección francesa pero era un combinado que no había contado con la aprobación de la FER"
  - url: https://ferugby.es/Descargar/1045/05-palmares/86559/anuario-21-22-parte-4-historial-competiciones-internacionales.pdf
  - quote: "9 junio 1929 España Alemania 15-24 Barcelona (Exposición Internacional)"
  - status: VERIFIED

- **Aleu — year verified, match-day office unresolved:** FER's presidents list puts Enrique Gutiérrez in 1928–1929 and Baudilio Aleu in 1929–1931. The exact handover date is not established. The OCR of *El Mundo Deportivo*'s 21 May 1929 report appears to call Gutiérrez federation president; it is too garbled to elevate that phrase to a verified quotation. Do not stage Aleu as president at the stadium without a cleaner contemporary source.
  - url: https://ferugby.es/juan-carlos-martin-hansen-elegido-como-nuevo-presidente-de-ferugby/
  - quote: "D. Enrique GUTIÉRREZ  (1928-1929)"
  - quote: "D. Baudilio ALEU TORRES (1929-1931)"
  - status: VERIFIED

- **Pedraja and the Spanish XV — unresolved:** *La Vanguardia* names Pedraja as the second try scorer but omits him from its printed starting XV. BUC's club history appears to agree, but the page timed out during verification and is not evidence here. The original *El Mundo Deportivo* report appears to describe Pedraja replacing Mongrell after Nubiols went off; its column-mixed OCR is insufficient for a clean exact quotation. Treat the printed XV as a starting list, not proof that Pedraja did not play. Spanish club affiliations remain **NOT FOUND**.

- **Italian names — unresolved transcription:** The *La Vanguardia* transcription prints “Modonessi,” “Barraghi,” “Briechi” and “Paselli (0)”; the original *El Mundo Deportivo* OCR is too damaged to adjudicate spellings or the number. Preserve the newspaper's printed forms, marked as such, until a legible scan is checked.

- **Milan 1930 — verified core, missing detail:** 29 May, 3–0, Milan, Italy's first win and second Test are verified by FIR. The ground was the Arena in Milan according to the Italian athletics federation's retrospective. The scorer, XVs, referee, and a usable contemporary Milan match voice are **NOT FOUND** in accessible primary material; do not infer the scorer from tertiary pages.
  - url: https://www.federugby.it/storias/
  - quote: "29 maggio 1930, Secondo test-match per la Nazionale, che a Milano centra la prima vittoria della propria storia battendo 3-0 la Spagna."
  - url: https://www.fidal.it/upload/files/ATLETICArivista/atletica_6_2006.pdf
  - quote: "Sul prato dell’Arena ha debuttato in Italia la nazionale di rugby"
  - status: VERIFIED

- **First two meetings — verified within both unions' match lists:** Barcelona 1929 and Milan 1930 precede the next listed Spain–Italy match in 1951. No current all-time total is asserted.

## Readiness test (scene, contemporary voice, institution)

- **Scene: READY for Barcelona 1929.** The date, exact stadium, Exposition-programme placement, 9–0 result, scoring sequence, referee, ceremony, crowd estimate and royal interruption have contemporary support. Keep the crowd figure attributed to *La Vanguardia*, not as an independently counted attendance.
- **Contemporary voice: READY.** *La Vanguardia* supplies play-by-play and ceremony. A contrasting *La Veu de Catalunya* account says the crowd was mainly drawn by football, not rugby: “La gent no anà pas a l’Estadi pel rugby.” It also criticises the press accommodation. That contrast prevents the stadium crowd from being mistaken for 60,000 rugby devotees.
  - url: https://www.fundaciobarcelonaolimpica.es/wp-content/uploads/sites/3/2019/07/AH_1_expo-universal-1929_WEB.pdf
  - quote: "La gent no anà pas a l’Estadi pel rugby."
  - status: VERIFIED
- **Institution: READY with limits.** The Exposition organising committee's sports commission and FIR's nascent federation are documented; Giorgio Vaccaro's election as FIR president on 20 September 1928 is established. FER's presidency changed during 1929, but Aleu's exact status on match day is unresolved. FIR calls Barcelona its first Test and Milan its second and first win.
  - url: https://www.federugby.it/storias/
  - quote: "20 settembre 1928, Giorgio Vaccaro è eletto Presidente della neonata Federazione Italiana Rugby."
  - quote: "20 maggio 1929, A Barcellona la Nazionale disputa il primo test-match della propria storia, battuta 9-0 dalla Spagna."
  - status: VERIFIED
- **Overall: READY for a Barcelona-centred scene, NOT READY for a detailed Milan return-match scene.** The latter has firm date, ground, result and institutional milestone, but no verified scorer, teams or contemporary voice.
