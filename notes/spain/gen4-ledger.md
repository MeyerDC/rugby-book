## FIRA competitions, 1990–1997 (Spain's lines and the season summaries)

- **fact:** Spain in FIRA through the decade: promoted in October 1990; top group 1990–92, fifth of five and relegated, though it beat Romania 6–0 in Madrid on 4 April 1992 and lost to Italy by a point (21–22) on 9 February 1992; a preliminary tournament in 1992–93 (lost 0–52 to Italy in Madrid) and promotion by a play-off against Morocco at Toulouse, 28 November 1993; top group 1993–94, fifth of five and relegated again; first in the second division in 1994–95 (90–8 over the Czech Republic); second in its group in 1995–96, with a 9–81 home defeat by France at Sant Boi on 31 March 1996.
