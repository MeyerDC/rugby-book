## Errors (fact, arithmetic, contradiction, logic)

1. **King**: "Outside Barcelona, rugby's first trials had been at the Escuela Central de Gimnasia". This contradicts Vigo 1910, Coruña 1911, the Metropolitano and Tolosa, all told earlier. Fix: "Outside Catalonia, the first Spaniards to take the game up in earnest…"
2. **King**: "In Madrid it was the football men who took it up, as it had been in Barcelona". The paragraph that follows names English enthusiasts, a Czech, a gymnastics society and cadets, and says Real Madrid's side had nothing to do with the football team. Fix: "taken up under the football clubs' names, though not by their footballers."
3. **King**: "By the end of that year [1924] four Castilian clubs were playing: Real Madrid…", but Real Madrid's first match was on 10 January 1925. Fix: "had teams".
4. **King**: "It had nothing to do with the club's football team … since most of its players were foreigners". The reason doesn't follow, and it repeats "its foreign members" from the sentence before. Fix: "Real Madrid's in name only; most of its players were foreigners."
5. **King**: 1926 as "the federation's second championship" leaves 1924–25 unexplained, and "cost the championship three seasons" implies the quarrel was the only gap. Fix: add "none had been played in 1924 or 1925".
6. **Nineteen Teams**: "By February 1923 the federation had six affiliated clubs … though the count given was seven". "The federation" is ambiguous (Spanish or Catalan?), and the six/seven point is a source quibble. Fix: "the Spanish federation listed six…"; move the discrepancy to Sources.
7. **Nineteen Teams**: "Some of the federation's work plainly began before its constitution did". That holds only if the 1923 date is right. Fix: "If the later date is right, …"
8. **Nineteen Teams**: the CADCI is among the thirteen clubs "by the end of 1922", but its rugby committee announced itself in January 1923. Fix: "whose rugby committee announced itself publicly in January 1923".
9. **Nineteen Teams**: Reynard coaches the "Unió Sportiva Sants" in the 1922 paragraph; the "Unió Deportiva de Sants" takes up the game in 1923. Fix: use one name, and drop Sants from the 1922 coaching list.
10. **King**: "FER" is used ("no sanction from the FER", "the old FER") but never introduced. Fix: "the Spanish federation".
11. **Veterinarian / Nineteen Teams**: the prose spells the names Vinçon and Lafitte, but Gibert's quote (now in English) gives Vençon and Laffite. Fix: standardise the English text; keep the source spelling only in the original.
12. **King**: "In Barcelona the game had belonged to clerks, students and professional men" forgets Sant Boi's bricklayers and peasants. Fix: add them.
13. **Veterinarian**: Toulouse "by the end of the 1910s" a model for France cannot explain enthusiasm Aleu took away in 1913. Fix: "a side that would become…"
14. **Too Rough**: "the city's sporting press spent the next decade explaining it". What follows explains rugby's rejection in general, not FC Barcelona's choice. Fix: "…explaining why the city was right to leave rugby alone."
15. **Too Rough**: the Royal Navy attribution to Torrebadella is a needless hedge, and he is introduced in full twice (Too Rough and Veterinarian). Fix: introduce him once.
16. **Veterinarian**: "What the club lacked was anyone to play" reads as no players, but the club had twenty-five members. Fix: "anyone to play against".
17. **Veterinarian**: "(in the paper's Castilian, the Unión Sportiva Perpignaise…)": "Perpignaise" is French. Fix: "in the paper's own forms".

Arithmetic, weekdays and the presidency sequence all check out.
