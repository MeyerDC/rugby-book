# Spain, Generation 2 — the Barcelona press (*La Vanguardia*, 1942–1954)

Generation-wide research, by hand in the main thread, 10 October 2026. **Retrieval finding: *La Vanguardia*'s archive serves each page as a PDF with an OCR text layer, open to curl**, at `http://hemeroteca-paginas.lavanguardia.com/LVE07/HEM/<YYYY>/<MM>/<DD>/LVG<YYYYMMDD>-<NNN>.pdf` (NNN = page, zero-padded). Its search form is closed to scripts (403/405), so a page is found by knowing the date and reading the issue. The paper did not publish on Mondays: a Sunday match is in Tuesday's issue.

**The OCR is rough.** Quotes below are short runs the OCR got right, copied exactly so the checker can find them. Where the fact rests on a garbled line it says "read by hand" and the author can open the PDF. The page texts are saved in `notes/spain/sources/lavanguardia-<date>-p<NN>.txt`. No chapter prose.

## 29 June 1941: the first Generalísimo's Cup final, found (Vallecas)

- **fact:** *La Vanguardia* of Sunday 29 June 1941, page 5, in its Madrid notes on that afternoon's football final of the "III Copa del Generalísimo" (Valencia v Español, at Chamartín, where it expects twenty-three thousand), passes on a Madrid paper's advice: supporters who cannot get in to the football "can make up for it by going to watch the rugby championship at Vallecas, between the teams of Español and Madrid's S.E.U." Read from the page image: "Añade que los aficionados pueden compensar el no poder ir a ver el partido de fútbol de la tarde, yendo a presenciar el campeonato de rugby en Vallecas, entre los equipos del «Español» y el madrileño del S.E.U." So the first rugby final under Franco's title was played **at Vallecas, in Madrid, on the weekend of the football cup final, by every sign the same day, 29 June 1941**, and Español of Barcelona had a side in both.
  - url: http://hemeroteca-paginas.lavanguardia.com/LVE07/HEM/1941/06/29/LVG19410629-005.pdf
  - quote: "peonato da rugby en Valleeas, entre los"
  - quote: "equipos del «Español» y el madrileño del"
  - quote: "III Copa del Generalísimo, ha definido en"
  - quote: "son mas, muíaos más de los veintitrés mil"
  - status: VERIFIED
  - note: the paper gives no kick-off time and does not use the word "final" for the rugby; the yearbook supplies that and the score (14–10 to the S.E.U.). "The same day" is the natural reading of the sentence, not a stated fact: write "that weekend" or "the day of the football final, by the paper's account". No report of the rugby match itself was found in the issues of 29 June or 1 July.

- **fact:** *La Vanguardia* of 1 July 1941, page 5, reports the football final: Valencia beat Español ("El gran ataque del Valencia triunfó en los primeros veinticinco minutos"). Español's club therefore lost both of the Generalísimo's cup finals it contested in Madrid that weekend, the football one to Valencia and the rugby one to the S.E.U.

  - quote: "C. de F. Valencia, 3"

## 24 June 1942: the second Generalísimo's Cup final (Chamartín)

- **fact:** *La Vanguardia* of 25 June 1942 carried a Cifra agency note from Madrid dated the 24th: the final of the Spanish championship, which also carried the Copa de S.E. el Generalísimo, was played that afternoon at Chamartín between S.E.U. de Madrid and CF Barcelona, and Barcelona won 17–8 after clear superiority in both halves. The note says the win gave Barcelona its third title of the 1941–42 season. It names no cup-presenter and no crowd.
  - url: http://hemeroteca-paginas.lavanguardia.com/LVE07/HEM/1942/06/25/LVG19420625-007.pdf
  - quote: "de Chamartin se ha celebrado la"
  - quote: "obtuvo un claro triunfo por 17 a 8."
  - status: VERIFIED
  - note: confirms the date and ground given by FC Barcelona's own history (scout dossier), which adds Moscardó handing over the cup. Moscardó is in the club's account only.

## 12 October 1943: Franco at the Ciudad Universitaria

- **fact:** *La Vanguardia* of 13 October 1943, page 2, printed the same Cifra report as *La Nueva España* (scout dossier): Franco left his car at the bridge between the football and rugby pitches and watched part of a rugby match between a Barcelona side and S.E.U. de Madrid. So the scene has two printings of one agency text, not two witnesses. The Barcelona side's name is garbled in both ("F. D. F." / "F. B F"); read by hand, it may be the Frente de Juventudes or the Facultad de Derecho, and is **not established**.
  - url: http://hemeroteca-paginas.lavanguardia.com/LVE07/HEM/1943/10/13/LVG19431013-002.pdf
  - quote: "del de rugby, el Caudillo descendió de su"
  - status: VERIFIED
  - note: both printings name the man who received Franco at the Colegio Mayor Jiménez de Cisneros as its architect, "señor Barroso". The scout has a Javier Barroso as captain of Arquitectura and president of Atlético de Madrid. Same man: a lead, not established.

## 12 October 1943, more from *La Nueva España* (read directly; adds to the scout dossier)

- **fact:** The paper's headline and opening: the day was the Día de la Hispanidad, and the occasion "the solemn inauguration of the Ciudad Universitaria and of the academic year 1943–44". At the Hospital Clínico a flag at half-mast marked "the mourning for the fallen in that historic place", and a Cross of the Fallen eighteen metres high stood on the Faculty of Medicine's ground: the regime's own press recording the campus as a battlefield of the war. The buildings inaugurated that day were Philosophy and Letters, Pharmacy, Chemical Sciences, Architecture, "Deportes" and the School of Agronomists.

He left the campus at a quarter to seven. The crowd "filled the stands and ringed the pitch" and surrounded him.

## 5 May 1946: FC Barcelona keep the cup (Les Corts)

- **fact:** *La Vanguardia* of 7 May 1946, page 8, reported Sunday's final at Les Corts: CF Barcelona 23–13 S.E.U. de Madrid, the club's third title running, which gave it the trophy outright. Kick-off at 18:00; 3–0, 3–5, 6–5 in the first half; the report praises the good play, sportsmanship and "caballerosidad" of both fifteens over eighty minutes. Both line-ups are printed (OCR poor; read by hand at S1).
  - url: http://hemeroteca-paginas.lavanguardia.com/LVE07/HEM/1946/05/07/LVG19460507-008.pdf
  - quote: "de España de Rugby"
  - quote: "tanteo final de 23 a 13 a favor de los"
  - status: VERIFIED

- **fact:** The same report lists who attended: the "national" rugby directors (read by hand from a garbled line as **Chicheri, Blanco and Marín**: the FER's president of 1939–46 and, if the surnames are theirs, its presidents of 1949 and 1962) and, separately, "los de la Catalana": **Sentís, Císcar, Pascual and Baltasar**. So in May 1946 there was a Catalan federation with its own directors, sitting beside the national ones at a final. Whatever happened to it in 1940, it was not abolished.
  - url: http://hemeroteca-paginas.lavanguardia.com/LVE07/HEM/1946/05/07/LVG19460507-008.pdf
  - quote: "Sentís, Ciscar, Pascual y Baltasar"
  - status: VERIFIED
  - note: "Baltasar" is the Francisco Baltasar whom FC Barcelona's history names as its rugby section's delegate from 1924. Agrees with that history's "competiciones oficiales de la Federación Catalana de Rugby a principios de 1941".

## 6 May 1951: Rome, seen from Barcelona

- **fact:** *La Vanguardia* of 8 May 1951, page 18, has no report of Italy v Spain found on the page. It reports CF Barcelona 13–5 over a Frankfurt club at La Bordeta the same weekend, and explains the home side's flat performance partly by "the absence of some of its regulars, who were in Rome with the national side". The first Spanish fifteen in fifteen years was, at least in part, FC Barcelona's.
  - url: http://hemeroteca-paginas.lavanguardia.com/LVE07/HEM/1951/05/08/LVG19510508-018.pdf
  - quote: "nos de sus titulares que estaban en Roma"
  - quote: "con la selección nacional, y la excesiva"
  - status: VERIFIED
  - note: the other 23 pages of the issue were not searched beyond the word "rugby"; a Rome report may be under another heading or in the 7–9 May *Mundo Deportivo*.

## 13 April 1952: the first home international since 1936 (Montjuïc)

- **fact:** *La Vanguardia* of 15 April 1952, page 17, under the heading "Copa de Europa, en Montjuich": Italy beat Spain 6–0 in the Montjuïc stadium before "numeroso público"; pennants exchanged, both national anthems played; 0–0 at half-time; an Italian penalty goal ten minutes into the second half and a try by the wing at 22 minutes. The paper judged the Italians superior in preparation, technique and physical condition. Both line-ups are printed, and a club curtain-raiser (Natació Barcelona against a French side from Saint-Girons).
  - url: http://hemeroteca-paginas.lavanguardia.com/LVE07/HEM/1952/04/15/LVG19520415-017.pdf
  - quote: "Italia ganó a España, en Rugby, por 6 a 0"
  - quote: "Con asistencia de numeroso"
  - quote: "Barba, que realiió un excelente arbitraje,"
  - status: VERIFIED
  - note: Montjuïc is the ground of the 1929 Exposition match that opens Gen 1. Same stadium, same opponent, twenty-three years on, the result reversed.

## January 1940: the first foreign side after the war (Les Corts)

- **fact:** *La Vanguardia* of 2 January 1940, page 9, previews two matches at Les Corts on 6 and 7 January between a home selection and "la selección de Bucarest", after a last trial match on the previous Sunday that ended eight points each. It credits the federation's preparation of the players, speaks of "la forzosa inactividad de tres años", and calls the fixture the first visit of a foreign side and a first marker in the game's revival.
  - url: http://hemeroteca-paginas.lavanguardia.com/LVE07/HEM/1940/01/02/LVG19400102-009.pdf

  - quote: "equipo extranjero en nuestros campos y"
  - status: VERIFIED

- **fact:** *La Vanguardia* of 9 January 1940, page 6, reports the second match: Bucarest 3, Barcelona 3, a try each (Elías for Barcelona, "Argevez" for Bucharest), neither converted, the home side described as "nuestro equipo, representativo de la ciudad". The first match had gone against the home side ("el resultado no fue muy halagüeño"); no score for it is on the page. The sports page's headline: "Nuestro Rugby logró un brillante empate ante los rumanos". Both line-ups are printed; a **Baltasar** is in Barcelona's.

## Arquitectura's own history, read directly (adds to the scout dossier)

- **fact:** Before the war the School of Architecture's side, captained by **Carlos García San Miguel**, won the last two university championships. (The FER president listed for 1936 is Luis García San Miguel. Same family: not established.)
  - url: https://www.arquitectura-rugby.org/club/historia-club-deportivo-arquitectura/
  - quote: "lograría los dos últimos campeonatos universitarios hasta la Guerra Civil"
  - status: VERIFIED

- **fact:** The club's list of its leading players of the late 1940s includes Matamoros, Medel, Pasalodos, Rivas, Vázquez de Castro and Zuazo. **Matamoros, Medel and a Vázquez are in the S.E.U. de Madrid fifteen printed for the 1946 final**; a Zuazo ("Zuaso") is in the Spanish fifteen at Montjuïc in 1952; a Rivas and an Arganza are in the Spanish fifteen of 25 April 1954, and Nicolás Arganza captained Arquitectura. Same surnames in each case, and the club itself says its best men played both the university competition and for federated clubs. The S.E.U. de Madrid side was, at least in part, the faculties' players under the union's name.
  - url: https://www.arquitectura-rugby.org/club/historia-club-deportivo-arquitectura/
  - quote: "Matamoros, Medel, Mestre, Nácher, Pasalodos"
  - quote: "Rafael Zuazo"
  - quote: "Nicolás Arganza"
  - status: VERIFIED
  - note: identity by surname only. The prose may say the names recur; it may not say they are the same men.

- **fact:** In the 1960s the club's main rivals in university competition were "the Colegio Mayor Cisneros, the strongest side then", followed by San Pablo, Aquinas, Aparejadores, Ensenada and the Faculty of Sciences.
  - url: https://www.arquitectura-rugby.org/club/historia-club-deportivo-arquitectura/
  - quote: "Nuestros principales rivales en las competiciones universitarias fueron el Colegio Mayor Cisneros, el equipo más potente entonces"

  - url: https://www.arquitectura-rugby.org/club/historia-club-deportivo-arquitectura/
