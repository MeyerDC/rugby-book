# Spain, Generation 3 — the ledger (1966–1990)

Generation-wide research, by hand in the main thread, 10 October 2026, from the federation's yearbook (Anuario 2021-22). Presidents, every cup final, every league champion, every senior men's international. What the ledgers cannot say is in `gen3-scout.md` and `gen3-press.md`. No chapter prose. Quotes are the yearbook's own lines (asterisks dropped; where a league line prints two seasons side by side it is quoted as printed).

## Presidents

- **fact:** The FER's list has Florencio Noriega Ruiz from 1967, Javier Cortázar Cano from 1977, Alberto Pico Marín from 1981 and Antonio Moreno Romo de Arce from 1992, all listed as Madrid; two acting presidents fall in the period, Javier Barroso Ladrón de Guevara (1980) and Antonio Martín Sánchez (1989), also Madrid. The list gives years and a city, nothing on how each took office.
