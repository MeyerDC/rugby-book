## Baldiri Aleu: family, Toulouse, and the return

- **fact:** Baldiri (Baudilio) Aleu Torres was born in Sant Boi de Llobregat on 9 November 1893 and died in Castelldefels on 26 May 1975.
  - url: https://www.enciclopedia.cat/esportpedia/baldiri-aleu-torres
  - quote: "(Sant Boi de Llobregat, Baix Llobregat, 9 de novembre de 1893 — Castelldefels, Baix Llobregat, 26 de maig de 1975)"
  - status: VERIFIED
- **fact:** Encouraged by his father, Aleu travelled to Toulouse to begin veterinary studies in 1911; a surviving matriculation record ("Feuille Matricule") from the École Nationale Vétérinaire de Toulouse dates his enrolment to 16 October 1911.
  - url: https://polired.upm.es/index.php/materiales_historia_deporte/article/view/4938/6217
  - quote: "Tiempo atrás, durante su preadolescencia y alentado por su padre , Aleu emprendió un viaje a … Toulouse para iniciar sus estudios de veterinaria en 1911."
  - quote: "École Nationale Vétérinaire de Toulouse, «Feuille Matricule. Aleu, Baudilio», 16 de octubre de 1911, École Nationale Vétérinaire de Toulouse."
  - status: VERIFIED (the school archive record itself was not read directly; cited via the article's footnote). Note: the article says "preadolescencia", but with the encyclopedia's birth date (9 November 1893) he was 17 at enrolment. Don't write "as a boy".
- **fact:** Toulouse was, in the article's words, "an important rugby enclave of southern France," and it was there that Aleu discovered, played and became familiar with the sport.
  - url: https://polired.upm.es/index.php/materiales_historia_deporte/article/view/4938/6217
  - quote: "Fue en aquel lugar, importante enclave … de rugby del sur de Francia, donde descubrió, practicó y se familiarizó con el nuevo deporte."

## Stade Toulousain and the École Vétérinaire: the model Aleu brought back

- **fact:** UE Santboiana's own history explicitly ties Aleu's enthusiasm to the university game at Toulouse and to Stade Toulousain itself, which it says grew out of university groups and was already "a reference across all of France" by the late 1910s.
  - url: https://www.uesantboiana.com/es/historia.cfm
  - quote: "L'Stade Toulousain campió d'Europa 2010, es va constituir amb grups universitaris i ja al final dels anys deu del segle XX era un referent a tot França."
  - quote: "Segurament l'entusiasme de Baldiri Aleu va sorgir dels partits universitaris i del mateix equip tolosenc."
  - status: VERIFIED
- **fact:** The same club history stresses that in that era, to study veterinary medicine properly, one had to study at Toulouse — "the closest city and focal point of culture in southern Europe."
  - url: https://www.uesantboiana.com/es/historia.cfm
  - quote: "Estem parlant d'una època en la qual per a estudiar veterinària en condicions, s'havia d'estudiar a Tolosa, la ciutat més propera i focus de cultura al sud d'Europa."
  - status: VERIFIED
- **fact:** Toulouse's veterinary school had its own rugby club, the Union Sportive de l'École Vétérinaire (USEV), founded in 1899 (grey shirts, black shorts), which in 1905 merged with the Sport Athlétique Toulousain to form "Véto-Sport"; in May 1907 Véto-Sport merged with the Stade Olympien des Étudiants de Toulouse, and in 1908 the combined club was renamed Stade Toulousain.
  - url: https://arvt.kalisport.com/dossiers/article/1/le-rugby-a-l-ecole-veterinaire-de-toulouse-de-ses-origines-a-la-creation-du-stade-toulousain
  - quote: "En 1899, un certain Coulon, joueur du SOET, décide de se séparer du SOET pour créer l'Union Sportive de l'Ecole Vétérinaire (USEV), qui jouera en maillot gris et culotte noire."
  - quote: "En 1905, face à une équipe du SOET plus redoutable que jamais, l'USEV et le SAT s'associent pour former le Véto-Sport."
  - quote: "Enfin, c’est en mai 1907 que le SOET et le Véto-Sport consentent à rassembler leurs sociétés"
  - quote: "C'est au cours de l'année 1908 que la nouvelle association sera renommée sous le nom plus digeste de Stade Toulousain."
  - status: VERIFIED
- **fact:** From the merger onward, veterinary-school players were central to Stade Toulousain's rise: Octave Léry, François Moura, François-Xavier Dutour and Pierre Jauréguy were among the "Vétos" who starred when the Stade became French champion for the first time in 1912; Émile Larrouy and Pierre Pons (later a France international) were veterinary-school players in the Stade's 1922 title — the very years Aleu was a student there and just after.
  - url: https://arvt.kalisport.com/dossiers/article/1/le-rugby-a-l-ecole-veterinaire-de-toulouse-de-ses-origines-a-la-creation-du-stade-toulousain

  - quote: "Ces derniers se sont illustrés notamment lorsque le Stade est devenu champion de France pour la première fois en 1912"
  - quote: "La génération d'après-guerre aura également vu les vétérinaires Émile Larrouy et Pierre Pons participer à la victoire du Stade en 1922, première d'une longue série."
  - quote: "Ce dernier sera même sélectionné avec l'équipe de France pour participer au Tournoi des 5 Nations."

## Sant Boi's civic associations and Aleu's later career

- **fact:** UE Santboiana's history frames its own founding as part of a wider wave of civic associationism in Sant Boi in the same period — sports clubs, cultural ateneus, theatre groups, choral societies — under the banner of culture, common effort and the formation of "body and spirit."
  - url: https://www.uesantboiana.com/es/historia.cfm
  - quote: "que duia a la societat civil a la creació de Clubs esportius, Ateneus Culturals, grups teatrals, masses corals, etc., sempre amb la bandera de la cultura, l'esforç comú i la formació del cos i l'esperit."
  - status: VERIFIED
- **fact:** Aleu was president of UE Santboiana from its founding until 1936, and president of the Catalan Rugby Federation once its statutes were approved in September 1922.
  - url: https://www.enciclopedia.cat/esportpedia/baldiri-aleu-torres
  - quote: "Fundà la Unió Esportiva Santboiana (1921), club degà a Catalunya i a l'Estat espanyol, i en fou president fins el 1936."
  - url: https://www.uesantboiana.com/es/historia.cfm
  - quote: "El setembre de 1922 foren aprovats els estatuts de la Federació Catalana de Rugby pel Govern Civil de Barcelona, amb Baldiri Aleu com a president."
  - status: VERIFIED

## Conflicts

- **Surname: Aleu Torras vs Aleu Torres.** UE Santboiana's own site and Torrebadella (2020) both write "**Baldiri Aleu Torras**." The Catalan encyclopedia (enciclopedia.cat), the Sant Boi local-history blog and the FER's own Anuario all write "**Aleu Torres**" (the federation's Latinised form is "Baudilio Aleu Torres"). The book should decide which spelling to standardise on; "Torres" has the edge in independent, dated biographical sources.
- **Birth year: 1893 vs 1894.** Enciclopedia.cat and the Sant Boi blog both give **9 November 1893** (or simply "1893"). Torrebadella (2020) gives "**Baldiri Aleu Torras (1894-1975)**." Death year (1975) is not in dispute.
- **Death place: Castelldefels vs Sant Boi.** Enciclopedia.cat states Aleu died in **Castelldefels**, 26 May 1975. The Sant Boi local-history blog states he "died in the same town" — i.e., **Sant Boi** — in 1975. Neither source was contradicted by a third.
- **The manifesto's title and year.** Three sources describe a Baldiri Aleu manifesto in *La Veu de Catalunya*: UE Santboiana's site titles it "**Entorn el Futbol-Rugby**," dated **1922**; the Catalan encyclopedia titles it "**Entorn del foot-ball rugby**," also dated 1922; and Torrebadella's bibliography cites a differently-titled Aleu article, "**En torn del Futbol Rugby**," dated **28 November 1921**, which he describes as a call for new clubs rather than a general manifesto. These may be the same piece misdated by one source, two different Aleu articles conflated by the others, or (most likely, given how close "Entorn"/"En torn" are typographically) the same article that both a 1921 and a 1922 tradition have attached different years to. The primary text was not reached to settle it.
- **Reynard: fellow student, or a stranger met in 1921?** A 1923 *La Jornada Deportiva* tribute, as reported by Torrebadella, calls Michel Reynard "**compañero de estudios de Aleu**" (Aleu's fellow student) — implying they knew each other from Toulouse. Pasarello, Pujadas and Martín (2023), working from UE Santboiana's own archive, instead date their **first meeting to May 1921 in Barcelona**, at the Perpignan–Narbonne exhibition matches, with Reynard introduced as a French engineer resident in the city, not a Toulouse classmate. The archival account (with a dated letter) is the stronger claim; the "fellow student" framing may be journalistic shorthand for "both had a French sporting education."
- **Reynard vs Reinard.** Every academic and archival source spells the mentor's name "**Reynard**." The English-language *ara.cat* piece on a 2023 documentary spells it "**Reinard**" throughout its Reynard-focused paragraph — likely a translation or transcription slip, but the name appears that way on the live page.
- **When did Aleu become FER president?** Pasarello, Pujadas and Martín (2023) write that Aleu helped found the FER in 1922, becoming its "**primer tesorero y presidente de la institución, ya en 1925**" (first treasurer, and president as early as 1925). The federation's own Anuario presidents list instead records "**Baudilio ALEU TORRES Barcelona (1923)**" as president immediately, with Enrique Gutiérrez of Madrid president from 1928, before Aleu returns as president "**(1929)**" — no 1925 presidency appears in the federation's own document.
- **The Perpignan–Narbonne exhibition: May or June 1921?** Pasarello et al. date it to **15 and 16 May 1921**, footnoted to *L'Auto* (17 May 1921), *Le Figaro* (18 May 1921) and the Barcelona press of 10–11 May. UE Santboiana's site says **June 1921**, at Espanyol's ground. The press-footnoted May date is the stronger one; the club history also puts the exhibition *before* the founding, which fits either date.
- **The Catalan federation: September 1922 or 1923?** UE Santboiana: statutes approved by the Civil Government in **September 1922**, with Aleu as president. Pasarello et al.: founded in the **first half of 1923**. The two may describe different steps (statutes approved, then constituted). This belongs to subplot 3; recorded here because Aleu's presidency depends on it.
- **"Founded" 4 July 1921, or already organised months earlier?** The club's formal founding act is dated 4 July 1921, and its minute book begins the same day. But *La Veu de Catalunya* had already referred to "the twenty-five members belonging to the Unió Esportiva Sanboiana" on **18 April 1921**, Reynard wrote to Aleu about the new club on **12 May 1921**, and training had begun "in the first third of 1921" — while the playing field itself was not formally leased until **September 1921**. The 4 July date is the act of incorporation, not the moment the group first called itself a club or began playing.
