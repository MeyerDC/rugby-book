# Spain, Generation 1 — Subplot 3: “Who Spoke for Spain” (1931–1936)

S1 research dossier. Scope: the domestic and international verdict on Barcelona and Madrid, the rise of Basque clubs, and the Civil War hinge. Exact quotations are in the source language; commentary is outside quotation marks. No chapter prose.

- **fact:** A study of military sport during the Second Republic says the Toledo Infantry Academy rugby team formed in the 1920s but disappeared early. Toledo is therefore a precursor, not an evidenced continuously active Madrid carrier through 1936.

## FIRA: foundation and international representation

- **fact:** The Five/Six Nations tournament organiser says France was ejected in 1931 and the tournament reverted to four Home Nations for 1932–39. This is sufficient context for France seeking a continental fixture network; it is not evidence that Spain itself played the French senior XV in these years.
  - url: https://www.sixnationsrugby.com/en/m6n/championship-history-mens
  - quote: "France were ejected from the tournament in 1931, which reverted back to being the Home Nations from 1932 through to 1939."
  - status: VERIFIED

- **fact:** Rugby Europe, FIRA's successor, dates its creation to 1934.
  - url: https://www.rugbyeurope.eu/about-us/about-rugby-europe
  - quote: "Rugby Europe was created in 1934"
  - status: VERIFIED

- **fact:** The Italian federation's historical timeline instead says FIRA was constituted at Turin in 1933 with Italy, France, Germany, Romania and Czechoslovakia as founders. This conflicts with Rugby Europe's 1934 date and with the researched Paris meeting; it must remain visible as a conflict, potentially referring to a preliminary gathering.

  - status: CONFLICT

It records the Catalan chair Francesc Massana at the meeting and says Catalonia was acknowledged with full rights, confirmed at Hanover in March.

## Readiness test (scene, contemporary voice, institution)

- **Scene — READY.** The 5 January 1934 *El Mundo Deportivo* interview supplies a grounded aftermath scene: Boix in Barcelona, Massana still returning from Paris, the invitation, instructions and powers, Catalonia's acceptance, and Boix's satisfaction and caution. It answers the international half of the subplot's question directly. The domestic finals and 1936 Test remain result runs rather than separate scenes.

- **Contemporary voice — READY.** Boix speaks in the 5 January interview. His "No, no me ha sorprendido" and insistence that he would say no more before Massana returned give both reaction and restraint. The 29 March 1936 *La Voz* notice adds a second, thinner period voice for the closing match.

- **Institution — yes, with a bounded conflict.** FER presidents and finals are evidenced, and the FER and Catalan-federation accounts support both Catalan and Spanish representation in FIRA during 1934. The precise founding and accession instruments remain unresolved; avoid assigning a sole seat or a definitive founding day.
