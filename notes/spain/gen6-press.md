## Brussels, 18 March 2018: El Español read in full

- **fact:** El Español's report of 18 March 2018 says the Spanish federation, after beating Romania, had gone through the channels to have Rugby Europe name officials of another nationality and met the refusal of Rugby Europe's president, Octavian Morariu, 'also Romanian'; that the Spanish camp had nevertheless insisted on the referee's honesty beforehand; and that Spain 'lost its nerve at the end of the match'. Santos in full: 'They did not let us play; the referee cut our play at every moment. The difference in penalties for and against was overwhelming. When we were coming back they cut us off again and again.' Nava in full: 'I do not want to think that rugby has prostituted itself to leave us out. When they assigned us the Romanian referees it struck us, but this is rugby, certain values are presumed.' At that point Spain expected to go on to a play-off, 'first against Portugal and then Samoa'.
