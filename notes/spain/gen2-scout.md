# Spain Generation 2 scout: "The Generalísimo's Cup" (July 1936 – 1965)

Scope: a generation-wide scouting pass on what the Franco regime made of a game that had been Catalan; the already-verified yearbook facts are not repeated here.

## 1. The S.E.U. and rugby

- **fact:** The S.E.U. was tied closely to Falange from its beginnings in 1933.
  - url: https://www.eduardomontagut.es/mis-articulos/historia/item/2345-el-seu.html
  - quote: "El Sindicato estaba estrechamente vinculado a Falange."
  - status: VERIFIED

- **fact:** During the Civil War the S.E.U. was refounded by a decree of 21 April 1937.
  - url: https://www.eduardomontagut.es/mis-articulos/historia/item/2345-el-seu.html
  - quote: "fue refundado por un Decreto de 21 de abril de 1937"
  - status: VERIFIED

- **fact:** After the war, the Madrid School of Architecture's club says the competition resumed under the S.E.U.'s protection, and that Manuel López-Mateos rebuilt the team.
  - url: https://www.arquitectura-rugby.org/club/historia-club-deportivo-arquitectura/
  - quote: "Tras la Guerra Civil, la competición se reanudó al amparo del Sindicato Español Universitario."
  - quote: "Manuel López-Mateos recompuso el equipo y lo capitaneó varias temporadas"
  - status: VERIFIED

- **fact:** The same club history says many of its prewar players had disappeared in the war or gone into exile, and names the architect Félix Candela among them.
  - url: https://www.arquitectura-rugby.org/club/historia-club-deportivo-arquitectura/
  - quote: "Muchos habían desaparecido en la guerra o en el exilio y nunca volverían al equipo, como Félix Candela"
  - status: VERIFIED

- **fact:** The club recruited its players in the early 1940s "aula por aula" (classroom by classroom), which suggests a game with no ready supply of players in the postwar university.
  - url: https://www.arquitectura-rugby.org/club/historia-club-deportivo-arquitectura/
  - quote: "Los inicios fueron duros, reclutando jugadores aula por aula"
  - status: VERIFIED

- **fact:** The club's history shows an Architecture side as university champion in 1947 wearing the S.E.U. kit, captained by Manolo López-Mateos.
  - url: https://www.arquitectura-rugby.org/club/historia-club-deportivo-arquitectura/
  - quote: "Equipo de Arquitectura Campeón Universitario en 1947 con la equipación del SEU"
  - status: VERIFIED

- **fact:** Architecture's university rivals were other faculties, and it also played non-university competitions including a "Trofeo Chicheri" (the surname of the first postwar FER president).
  - url: https://www.arquitectura-rugby.org/club/historia-club-deportivo-arquitectura/
  - quote: "En la competición universitaria nuestros rivales eran Medicina, Agricultura, Derecho, Odontología, Caminos, ICAI e Industriales."
  - quote: "(Copa de Navidad, Trofeo Chicheri)"
  - status: VERIFIED

- **fact:** The club says the best players alternated between the university competition and federated clubs such as Atlético or Real Madrid, so the S.E.U. league and the federation's Castile championship were two separate ladders.
  - url: https://www.arquitectura-rugby.org/club/historia-club-deportivo-arquitectura/
  - quote: "jugadores alternaban su participación en la competición universitaria con su militancia en los equipos entonces federados como el Atlético o el Real Madrid"
  - status: VERIFIED

- **fact:** Architecture says it won every Madrid university championship from 1948 to 1959.
  - url: https://www.arquitectura-rugby.org/club/historia-club-deportivo-arquitectura/
  - quote: "Arquitectura logró año tras año todos los Campeonatos Universitarios de Madrid desde 1948 hasta 1959"
  - status: VERIFIED

- **fact:** The S.E.U. pushed the rebuilding of the Ciudad Universitaria sports grounds from November 1940 and organised the first University Games there on 20 April 1942.
  - url: https://guspuente.wordpress.com/2013/11/21/estadio-nacional-de-rugby-nuestra-central-complutense/
  - quote: "En noviembre de 1940 comenzaron las obras de reconstrucción alentadas por el Sindicato Español Universitario (SEU)"
  - quote: "El 20 de abril de 1942 se realizaron en las instalaciones los primero Juegos Universitarios organizados por el SEU"
  - status: VERIFIED

- **fact:** The stadium complex was known as the "campos de deporte del SEU".
  - url: https://guspuente.wordpress.com/2013/11/21/estadio-nacional-de-rugby-nuestra-central-complutense/
  - quote: "llamado también campos de deporte del SEU"
  - status: VERIFIED

- **fact:** In the 1946 semi-final FC Barcelona beat a "Frente de Juventudes de Madrid" side (the Falange youth organisation) 9–3, so the party fielded rugby sides through more than one organisation.
  - url: https://rugbyfcb.wordpress.com/historia/
  - quote: "elimina al Frente de Juventudes de Madrid al vencerle por 9 a 3"
  - status: VERIFIED

- **fact:** Rugby at Valladolid began with the S.E.U. in the 1940s, playing teams from Zaragoza and Madrid.
  - url: https://www.diariodevalladolid.es/deportes/160526/138210/cura-frances-sembro-rugby.html
  - quote: "la gran llama del rugby pucelano fue encendida por el SEU Valladolid, con partidos que le enfrentaron a conjuntos de Zaragoza y de Madrid"
  - status: VERIFIED

- **fact:** The same article says those early sides were not really teams but all-round sportsmen gathered by the enthusiasm of a few; it names Pepe Rojo (the ground in Valladolid carries his name) as one of the first players.
  - url: https://www.diariodevalladolid.es/deportes/160526/138210/cura-frances-sembro-rugby.html
  - quote: "eran aficionados al deporte en general, innovadores que decidían practicar varios deportes a la vez"
  - quote: "ya formó parte de aquellos primeros equipos del SEU"
  - status: VERIFIED

- **fact:** A photograph captioned in 1963 still shows Pepe Rojo with his "SEU de Valladolid" team, so a S.E.U. rugby side existed up to two years before the 1965 reform.
  - url: https://www.diariodevalladolid.es/deportes/241113/265053/nombre-rugby.html
  - quote: "con su equipo SEU de Valladolid en 1963"
  - status: VERIFIED

- **fact:** The S.E.U. was not dissolved outright in 1965. A decree of 5 April 1965 created Professional Student Associations which, "though integrated in the S.E.U.", were in effect meant to replace it.
  - url: https://www.eduardomontagut.es/mis-articulos/historia/item/2345-el-seu.html
  - quote: "Un decreto del 5 de abril de 1965 creó las Asociaciones Profesionales de Estudiantes (APE), que aunque integradas en el SEU, en realidad, venían a sustituirlo."
  - status: VERIFIED

- **fact:** The BOE record gives the decree as Decreto 818/1965, of 5 April, published 7 April 1965, and notes that it was derogated in 1968.
  - url: https://www.boe.es/diario_boe/txt.php?id=BOE-A-1965-6644
  - quote: "Decreto 818/1965, de 5 de abril, por el que se regulan las asociaciones profesionales de estudiantes."
  - quote: "Fecha de publicación: 07/04/1965"
  - quote: "Fecha de derogación: 13/10/1968"
  - status: VERIFIED

- **fact:** The BOE record also lists an Order of 5 June 1965 regulating a "Comisaría para el Sindicato Español Universitario", so the S.E.U. had an official existence after the April decree.
  - url: https://www.boe.es/diario_boe/txt.php?id=BOE-A-1965-6644
  - quote: "se regula la Comisaría para el Sindicato Español Universitario, por Orden de 5 de junio de 1965"
  - status: VERIFIED

- **fact:** What happened to the S.E.U. rugby clubs after 1965 (how S.E.U. de Madrid, S.E.U. de León and others were renamed or absorbed).

## 2. The Delegación Nacional de Deportes

- **fact:** The decree of 22 February 1941 establishing the Delegación Nacional de Deportes de F.E.T. y de las J.O.N.S. appeared in the BOE of 5 March 1941, no. 64.
  - url: https://www.filosofia.org/hem/dep/boe/9410305d.htm
  - quote: "Decreto de 22 de Febrero de 1941 por el que se establece la Delegación Nacional de Deportes de F. E. T. y de las J. O. N. S."
  - quote: "Madrid, 5 de marzo de 1941 número 64"
  - status: VERIFIED

- **fact:** The preamble gives the Falange state's reason: the party, as intermediary between society and state, should lead all sport, while "conserving what is healthy" in the existing clubs and federations.
  - url: https://www.filosofia.org/hem/dep/boe/9410305d.htm
  - quote: "conservando cuanto hay de sano y aprovechable en la iniciativa de Agrupaciones que cuentan con una brillante historia de servicios al deporte español"
  - quote: "Al Partido, como intermediario político entre la sociedad y el Estado"
  - status: VERIFIED

- **fact:** Article 1 hands the direction and promotion of Spanish sport to Falange, and Article 2 turns the Olympic Committee into the Delegación Nacional de Deportes.
  - url: https://www.filosofia.org/hem/dep/boe/9410305d.htm
  - quote: "La dirección y el fomento del deporte español se encomienda a Falange Española Tradicionalista y de las J. O. N. S."
  - status: VERIFIED

- **fact:** Article 4 gives the Delegación the power to appoint the presidents and vice-presidents of the national federations and to approve their statutes and fixtures.
  - url: https://www.filosofia.org/hem/dep/boe/9410305d.htm
  - quote: "Nombrar los Presidentes y Vicepresidentes de las Federaciones deportivas nacionales o Entidades análogas."
  - quote: "Aprobar los Estatutos y Reglamentos de las Federaciones, Sociedades y Entidades deportivas en general"
  - quote: "Aprobar y rectificar les calendarios deportivos"
  - status: VERIFIED

- **fact:** The FER's own list of presidents gives each of them as "Madrid" with a year, and lists Juan L. de Chicheri Ligues also as Presidente de Honor (1947).
  - url: https://ferugby.es/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/Parte-I-Estructura-FER-2013-14-Anuario-FERugby.pdf
  - quote: "D. Juan L. de CHICHERI LIGUES"
  - quote: "D. José María UNZETA BASTERRECHEA"
  - quote: "D. Eugenio MARTÍNEZ PASALODOS"
  - status: VERIFIED

- **fact:** Moscardó, as Delegado Nacional de Deportes, personally handed the Copa del Generalísimo to FC Barcelona's captain after the 1942 final in Madrid.
  - url: https://rugbyfcb.wordpress.com/historia/
  - quote: "recibió la Copa del S.E. el Generalísimo de manos del General Moscardó delegado nacional de Deportes"
  - status: VERIFIED

- **fact:** The Escuela Central de Gimnasia at Toledo, which Moscardó directed in 1936, published a "Foot-ball Rugby: Reglamento" in 1927 (the rulebook is cited in a peer-reviewed article); the only link to Moscardó's later rugby role is that his school printed the rules. Moscardó as director of the school appears in search summaries only (RECALLED, not read on a page).
  - url: https://dialnet.unirioja.es/descarga/articulo/8295556.pdf
  - quote: "Foot-ball Rugby: Reglamento"
  - status: VERIFIED

- **fact:** Biographies of López de Chicheri, Unzeta, Blanco Mongay, Martínez Pasalodos and Marín Ocón (military, Falangist or former players).
  - status: NOT FOUND

## 3. The Catalan federation after 1939

- **fact:** The Catalan federation's own history says Catalonia was a full FIRA member from 1934 and that this independence lasted until 1940.
  - url: https://rugby.cat/historia/
  - quote: "Aquesta independència es perllongà fins al 1940."
  - status: VERIFIED

- **fact:** Enciclopèdia Catalana says that after the war the federation lost its independence but kept playing friendlies from 1940 against national sides like France or Italy.
  - url: https://www.enciclopedia.cat/esportpedia/federacio-catalana-de-rugby
  - quote: "perdé la seva independència però seguí disputant partits amistosos, a partir del 1940, contra seleccions com França o Itàlia"
  - status: VERIFIED

- **fact:** The Catalan federation says its first friendly after the war was against Romania at Les Corts in January 1940, played "as a regional selection".
  - url: https://rugby.cat/historia/
  - quote: "El primer d’ell va ser a gener de 1940 a l’Estadi de Les Corts contra Romania"
  - quote: "com a selecció regional"
  - status: VERIFIED

- **fact:** Llibertat.cat dates the expulsion of the Catalan federation from FIRA to 16 September 1940, says the Spanish federation (which had entered FIRA through Catalan sponsorship) forced the removal, and says the Francoist victory "purged" Catalan rugby.
  - url: https://www.llibertat.cat/2023/02/1940-la-federacio-catalana-de-rugbi-es-expulsada-del-federacio-internacional-de-rugbi-amateur-54231
  - quote: "El 16 de setembre de 1940 la Federació Catalana de Rugbi és expulsada del Federació Internacional de Rugbi Amateur."
  - quote: "va obligar al màxim organisme del moment a suprimir la Federació Catalana"
  - quote: "es depurà el teixit de la pilota ovalada a Catalunya"
  - status: VERIFIED

- **fact:** The FER's own centenary article says the Catalan federation "sponsored" the Spanish federation's entry into FIRA in 1934, which is the other side of the same story.
  - url: https://ferugby.es/que-debes-saber-de-la-historia-del-rugby-en-espana-23-momentos-en-los-101-anos-de-la-fer/
  - quote: "fue la Federación Catalana quien, con toda la burocracia lista, apadrinó el ingreso de la FER en la FIRA"
  - status: VERIFIED

- **fact:** FC Barcelona's rugby section says that after the war its sections were reorganised, friendlies were played from mid-1940 at Montjuïc, Diagonal and Sant Boi, and "official competitions of the Federación Catalana de Rugby" began in early 1941 (its words, not confirmed elsewhere).
  - url: https://rugbyfcb.wordpress.com/historia/
  - quote: "a mediados del año 1940 empiezan a jugarse partidos amistosos en los campos del Estadio de Montjuich, Diagonal y San Baudilio"
  - quote: "dan inicio las competiciones oficiales de la Federación Catalana de Rugby a principios de 1941"
  - status: VERIFIED

- **fact:** The FC Barcelona section's president at the time was the Marqués de la Mesa de Asta, who supported the section "decisively".
  - url: https://rugbyfcb.wordpress.com/historia/
  - quote: "presidida por el Excmo. Señor Marqués de la Mesa de Asta"
  - status: VERIFIED

- **fact:** The Catalan daily Ara reports a claim from the Catalan federation's press head that in 30 years of Franco's regime Catalonia produced only one new rugby club.
  - url: https://en.ara.cat/sports/the-centenary-sport-in-catalonia-that-franco-wanted-to-let-die_130_5295331.html
  - quote: "In Catalonia, in 30 years of Franco's regime, from 1939 to 1970, only one new club was born."
  - status: VERIFIED

- **fact:** The same Ara article says the Catalan players' anti-fascist record includes "players who were militiamen" remembered in Cornellà.
  - url: https://en.ara.cat/sports/the-centenary-sport-in-catalonia-that-franco-wanted-to-let-die_130_5295331.html
  - quote: "In Cornellà they remember players who were militiamen."
  - status: VERIFIED

- **fact:** The FER's own centenary text frames the postwar divergence: a French model (a flowering of clubs) in Catalonia and a British model (growth of university clubs) in Madrid.
  - url: https://ferugby.es/que-debes-saber-de-la-historia-del-rugby-en-espana-23-momentos-en-los-101-anos-de-la-fer/
  - quote: "mientras en Cataluña se dio un modelo francés (florecimiento de clubes), en Madrid se dio un modelo británico (crecimiento de clubes universitarios)"
  - status: VERIFIED

- **fact:** Baldiri Aleu was born 9 November 1893 and died 26 May 1975, and was president of UE Santboiana until 1936.
  - url: https://www.enciclopedia.cat/esportpedia/baldiri-aleu-torres
  - quote: "Sant Boi de Llobregat, Baix Llobregat, 9 de novembre de 1893 — Castelldefels, Baix Llobregat, 26 de maig de 1975"
  - quote: "en fou president fins el 1936"
  - status: VERIFIED

- **fact:** What Aleu did after 1936 (war, exile, retirement) and what the Santboiana did during the war.
  - status: NOT FOUND

- **fact:** Whether rugby clubs were forced to Castilianise names (the football section of Sant Boi was reportedly renamed "Club de Fútbol Samboyano" in 1941, which was not read on a page and so is RECALLED, NOT VERIFIED; no rugby-club example found).

## 4. The Civil War years 1936–39

- **fact:** The FER's own centenary text says the Civil War "razed" the advances rugby had made.
  - url: https://ferugby.es/que-debes-saber-de-la-historia-del-rugby-en-espana-23-momentos-en-los-101-anos-de-la-fer/
  - quote: "la Guerra Civil arrasó con los avances que se habían dado en el rugby"
  - status: VERIFIED

- **fact:** The Ciudad Universitaria, where the university rugby ground was being built, became one of the bloodiest fronts of the war and its buildings were badly damaged.
  - url: https://guspuente.wordpress.com/2013/11/21/estadio-nacional-de-rugby-nuestra-central-complutense/
  - quote: "frentes mas cruentos de la guerra, lo que ocasionó además de numerosas bajas en ambos frentes, graves daños a los edificios ya construidos"
  - status: VERIFIED

- **fact:** A peer-reviewed article by Torrebadella says the rugby team of the Toledo Infantry Academy formed in the 1920s disappeared quickly because superiors and teachers labelled the game "bárbaro".
  - url: https://dialnet.unirioja.es/descarga/articulo/8295556.pdf
  - quote: "equipo de la Academia de Infantería de Toledo que se formó en los años veinte"
  - quote: "bárbaro, a pesar de no haber"
  - status: VERIFIED

- **fact:** The same article says a new rugby team appeared in 1935 at the Aviation base of Cuatro Vientos and played in the Madrid civil championship (a possible root of the air force link to Atlético, not established).
  - url: https://dialnet.unirioja.es/descarga/articulo/8295556.pdf
  - quote: "equipo de rugby en la Aviación de Cuatro Vientos, que participó en el campeonato"
  - status: VERIFIED

- **fact:** A blog comment (weak, unsourced) says Tavernes was Spanish runner-up in 1936 against "FUE Rugbi Club" and lost to Barcelona; the FER list in the brief has no cup played that season, so treat this as unreliable.
  - url: https://lacotorradelavall.blogspot.com/2010/03/el-tavernes-rugby-club-fa-75-anys.html
  - quote: "Tavernes va arribar al subcampionat de Espanya de Rugbi a l'any 1936 contra el FUE Rugbi Club"
  - status: VERIFIED

- **fact:** Any match played, any club's own account of the war years, players killed, grounds lost, and rugby in the Nationalist zone.
  - status: NOT FOUND

## 5. A scene and a contemporary voice

- **fact:** On 12 October 1943 Franco opened the Ciudad Universitaria and went from the School of Architecture to the sports ground, where he stepped from his car at the bridge that separates the football pitch from the rugby pitch; La Nueva España (the Falange's Oviedo provincial paper) printed it on 13 October 1943.

  - quote: "La Nueva España Organo provincial de la Falange Española Tradicionalista y de las JONS"

  - status: VERIFIED

- **fact:** In the stand Franco sat with the Minister of Education and the S.E.U.'s national chief and watched part of a rugby match between "Barcelona F. D. F." and S.E.U. de Madrid.
  - url: https://www.filosofia.org/hem/dep/lne/1943q13a.htm

  - status: VERIFIED

  - url: https://www.filosofia.org/hem/dep/lne/1943q13a.htm
  - quote: "A continuación, el Jefe del Estado se trasladó al Colegio Mayor Jiménez de Cisneros"
  - status: VERIFIED

- **fact:** FC Barcelona's own account of the 1942 final: semi-final at Vallecas on 21 June (28–0 over Real Madrid), final at Chamartín on 24 June, S.E.U. "a very strong team in those days" beaten 17–8.

## 6. Madrid's university game

- **fact:** The Cisneros club, which won the 1966–67 final, is described as founded in 1943 and the third-oldest in Spanish rugby after FC Barcelona and Santboiana.
  - url: https://madrilanea.com/2018/11/club-rugby-cisneros-75-anos-combinando-deporte-educacion/
  - quote: "El equipo blanquiazul, fundado en 1943"
  - status: VERIFIED

- **fact:** Architecture's history calls the Cisneros side "recuperado" (revived) and says many Architecture students played there.
  - url: https://www.arquitectura-rugby.org/club/historia-club-deportivo-arquitectura/
  - quote: "acabaron jugando en el recuperado equipo del Colegio Cisneros, donde abundaban los estudiantes de Arquitectura"
  - status: VERIFIED

- **fact:** Late-1940s Architecture grew so strong it founded two more sides, Acantos and Arquitrabe, for the students who wanted to play.
  - url: https://www.arquitectura-rugby.org/club/historia-club-deportivo-arquitectura/

## Conflicts

- Aleu's birth year: the brief gives 1894, Enciclopèdia Catalana gives 9 November 1893 (https://www.enciclopedia.cat/esportpedia/baldiri-aleu-torres).
- When the Catalan federation ended: FC Barcelona's rugby section says "official competitions of the Federación Catalana de Rugby" began in early 1941 (https://rugbyfcb.wordpress.com/historia/); Enciclopèdia Catalana says it lost its independence after the war (https://www.enciclopedia.cat/esportpedia/federacio-catalana-de-rugby); Llibertat.cat dates the FIRA expulsion to 16 September 1940 (https://www.llibertat.cat/2023/02/1940-la-federacio-catalana-de-rugbi-es-expulsada-del-federacio-internacional-de-rugbi-amateur-54231); the Catalan federation's own page says independence "lasted until 1940" (https://rugby.cat/historia/). A web-search summary (not read on a page) mentioned a decree of 22 February 1941 dissolving it; the only decree of that date I read is the Delegación Nacional de Deportes decree, which does not name rugby.
- Who founded the Catalan federation: Enciclopèdia Catalana says founded in 1922 with David Cuyàs Pujol as first president; rugby.cat says the statutes were approved in September 1922 "amb Baldiri Aleu com a president" (https://rugby.cat/historia/). The brief's list of FER presidents begins with Baudilio Aleu Torres, 1923.
- The last Generalísimo cup: FC Barcelona's page says its last win was in 1966, final "en el campo de la Santboiana ante el Universitario", 3–0 (https://rugbyfcb.wordpress.com/historia/); the brief's yearbook list has Canoe in 1965–66 and Cisneros in 1966–67.
- S.E.U. "dissolution": the S.E.U. was not dissolved on 5 April 1965; Decreto 818/1965 created associations that "replaced" it, and an Order of 5 June 1965 set up a Comisaría for it (BOE page above).
- Tavernes: a local blog says the lost final to Santboiana was in 1943–44; the brief's yearbook list puts it in 1942–43. The blog is weak and the yearbook should stand.
- Valladolid El Salvador: the Diario de Valladolid piece gives the priest's request as 1961; a web-search summary of the club's page gave 1960 (page not read).
